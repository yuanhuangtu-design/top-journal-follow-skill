摘要：Animals use coordinated facial movements to sample the environment, ingest nutrients, and communicate. Rodents produce rhythmic facial movements during spontaneous behavior and cognitive tasks. Measuring these movements precisely and linking them to neural activity remains challenging. We introduce face-rhythm, an unsupervised pipeline that combines markerless point-tracking, spectral analysis, and non-negative tensor decomposition to reduce facial video to a small set of interpretable components. Applied to videos of mice performing Pavlovian conditioning, a brain-machine interface task, and free behavior, face-rhythm recovers whisking, sniffing, licking, and subtler motifs. The resulting components are consistent across animals, decode task variables and internal states, and explain cortical activity with a low-rank representation. Facial motor cortex neurons are predicted well by a phase-invariant spectral transformation of movements above roughly 0.5 Hz, whereas slower movements retain a phase-variant representation. Face-rhythm compares favorably with deep-learning tracking and embedding methods while yielding an interpretable description of facial behavior.

Cortical and meningeal neuroimmune interactions contribute to the generation of migraine headaches.浏览
PMID 43061830 · Brain : a journal of neurology · 2026 11 20
作者：Erdener ŞE, Kaya Z, Dalkara T
链接：PubMed · DOI
命中检索：神经科学期刊周候选
发表形式：期刊论文（研究设计待核对）
中科院分区：2025版｜医学｜1区
JCR分区：2026发布版（2025数据）｜CLINICAL NEUROLOGY：Q1；NEUROSCIENCES：Q1
摘要：Migraine is a prevalent disorder that ranks as the second leading cause of disability among all diseases. Recent advancements have significantly enhanced our comprehension of its underlying pathophysiology. Growing evidence highlights complex neuroimmune signaling networks-particularly interactions between meningeal nociceptors and resident immune cells-as key contributors to headache generation. The clinical success of anti-calcitonin gene-related peptide (CGRP) antibodies underscores the importance of intercellular communication between the dural nociceptive nerves and adjacent resident immune cells such as macrophages in migraine headache. Notably, the intercellular inflammatory interplay is not confined to the meninges. Disturbances within the brain parenchyma potentially act as initiating triggers, as suggested by the presence of aura and premonitory symptoms preceding the headache. Recent experimental discoveries indicate that neuronal stress triggers an inflammatory signaling cascade that, through interactions with astrocytes and microglia, propagates to the meninges, ultimately inducing meningeal neurogenic inflammation and headache. This model is supported by converging experimental evidence and human imaging studies employing PET and quantitative MRI, which document the presence of changes compatible with inflammatory activity within the brain parenchyma and overlying meninges of patients with migraine with aura. Genetic studies identifying several inflammation-related genes and pathways as risk factors for migraine further corroborate this concept. Furthermore, cell-specific transcriptomic analyses of mouse brains have revealed astrocyte-selective activation of nuclear factor kappa B -regulated inflammatory transcriptional pathways after cortical spreading depolarization, the neurophysiological correlate of migraine aura. Owing to their extensive interface with the cerebrospinal fluid, astrocytes are uniquely positioned to release inflammatory mediators to activate meningeal nociceptors and immune cells. Insights from pain research suggest that neuroimmune interactions may be responsible for the widespread parenchymal increases in 18 kDa translocator protein (TSPO) PET signals observed in migraine with aura patients. This discovery also identifies TSPO as a potential player in migraine pathophysiology and novel drug target. In this review, by integrating cellular and molecular findings from peripheral pain and inflammation research, we examine the dynamic interactions between neurons and glia, as well as between meningeal nociceptors and immune cells in the context of migraine headache. This perspective provides a conceptual framework for understanding how nociceptors and glial cells coordinate to signal non-homeostatic brain states-initiating protective, tightly regulated responses without progressing to overt neuroinflammatory disease and provides novel insight into the findings of PET studies involving TSPO.

摘要：One hallmark of intelligent behavior is the ability to optimize how much time is devoted to decisions. Yet the principles governing decision timing remain unclear. Here, we developed a novel paradigm for directly comparing decision-making between mice and humans. This approach allowed us to combine widefield and 2-photon imaging in the mouse frontal cortex with optogenetic inhibition to examine the underlying mechanisms. We found that history-dependent refinement of sequences in the medial frontal cortex (MFC), versus a thresholding mechanism in the lateral frontal cortex (LFC), provides a unified logic for decision strategy on a trial-by-trial basis. These dynamics reflect reinforcement-learning updates that explain timing variability better than sensory evidence. Our findings provide a computational framework that unifies voluntary decision-making across species.

摘要：Myasthenia Gravis is a potentially life-threatening autoimmune disease of the neuromuscular junction with fluctuating muscle weakness and fatigability. In recent years, the therapeutic landscape has been notably reshaped by the introduction of new therapies. These agents, primarily acting through complement inhibition or neonatal Fc-receptor blockade, have demonstrated significant efficacy and favorable safety profiles in randomized clinical trials and real-world settings. Their advent has refined clinical management, particularly in refractory patients, leading to meaningful improvements in patients' quality of life and future expectations. Meanwhile, in this evolving therapeutic landscape, new challenges and uncertainties in clinical decision-making have been raised, as new therapies are now part of the standard care in Myasthenia Gravis. Differences in regulatory agency approvals and reimbursement policies across Europe result in heterogeneous access to therapies, complicating treatment decisions and limiting shared real-world experiences. Consequently, clinicians face complex dilemmas regarding optimal patient selection, treatment choice, and long-term management strategies. To address these issues, a panel of experts, including adult and pediatric neurologists and patient representatives, within the framework of the European Reference Network for Neuromuscular Diseases convened to develop consensus-based positions. The aim of this work was to identify, explore, and discuss key challenges associated with the use of new therapies and to propose a practical approach to support clinicians in this rapidly evolving field. Given the variability in clinical approach, treatment availability and reimbursement policies across European healthcare systems, this position paper seeks to provide guidance that are adaptable across diverse settings. A literature review was conducted, focusing on pivotal clinical trials and real-world evidence. Eleven key topics were identified and discussed, and for each, consensus statements were developed and refined through a modified Delphi process: patient assessment from both clinician and patient perspectives; the role of autoantibodies and optimization of serological testing; selection of immunotherapies; definitions of treatment response and failure in the context of new therapies; strategies for switching between therapies; predictors of treatment response; management of concomitant therapies, including corticosteroids and conventional immunosuppressant; discontinuation strategies; their potential role as early-line therapies; their applicability in ocular MG; and future therapeutic perspectives, including ongoing clinical trials. In conclusion, new therapies represent a major advance in Myasthenia Gravis care but also introduce significant complexity. This expert position paper provides a framework to support clinicians in decision-making and highlights the need for harmonized guidelines and further research to optimize their use in routine practice.

摘要：Immature adult-born dentate granule cells (abDGCs) show heightened synaptic and structural plasticity and preferentially contribute to hippocampal-dependent memory. Protracted maturation (neoteny) of abDGCs in higher mammals is posited to offset the decline in adult hippocampal neurogenesis by supporting increased plasticity. Here we found that selective, synchronous expansion of 4-week-old immature, but not 8-week-old mature, mouse abDGCs promotes recruitment of fast-spiking parvalbumin-expressing inhibitory interneurons (INs) in hippocampal CA3 and CA2, increases feed-forward inhibition, decreases social memory interference and enhances social memory consolidation. Our findings suggest that neoteny of abDGCs supports cognition by increasing feed-forward inhibition in DG-CA3 and DG-CA2 and promoting network oscillations that underlie memory consolidation.
The hippocampus as a latent diffusion engine for memory, perception and cognitive dysfunction.推荐
PMID 43049192 · Brain : a journal of neurology · 2026 11 18
作者：Jones DT, Breakspear M
链接：PubMed · DOI
命中检索：神经科学期刊周候选
发表形式：期刊论文（研究设计待核对）
中科院分区：2025版｜医学｜1区
JCR分区：2026发布版（2025数据）｜CLINICAL NEUROLOGY：Q1；NEUROSCIENCES：Q1
摘要：The hippocampus is a central cognitive hub whose dysfunction contributes to most neurodegenerative dementias. Despite extensive knowledge of its anatomy, physiology and circuitry, a unifying account that links hippocampal dynamics to cognition and clinical syndromes remains lacking. Drawing on systems neuroscience and recent advances in generative artificial intelligence, we conceptualize the hippocampus as a latent diffusion engine that compresses sensory, interoceptive and cognitive inputs into low-dimensional representations and regenerates percepts, episodic memories and imagined scenes for cortical integration. Travelling waves of cortico-hippocampal oscillations, aligned with large-scale functional gradients, schedule and structure this generative process and organize distributed neural activity into coherent, semantically grounded constructs. We formalize this proposal as stochastic latent oscillatory diffusion (SLOD), a biologically grounded framework that maps hippocampal-cortical computations onto specific anatomical substrates and dynamical processes. While we do not suggest that the hippocampus implements diffusion algorithms per se, we show that its computations can be decomposed into homologous operations including compression, stochastic exploration, conditioning and reconstruction, whose formal structure closely parallels that of latent diffusion models. Finally, we demonstrate how major neurodegenerative dementias, including the Alzheimer's disease spectrum, dementia with Lewy bodies and frontotemporal dementia, can be understood as selective breakdowns of distinct components of this generative architecture.

摘要：Experience-dependent changes in myelination are important for motor learning, with newly generated oligodendrocytes contributing to the formation of new myelin. However, motor memories decay over time and the underlying mechanisms remain largely unexplored. Here we show that motor memory decay is linked to changes in memory-associated myelin sheaths resulting from microglial phagocytosis in mouse brain. Mechanistically, reduced activity of motor memory engram cells lowers oligodendrocyte expression of the 'don't eat me' signal from Cluster of differentiation 47 (CD47), facilitating microglial phagocytosis and driving myelin loss. 摘要：Phenylketonuria (PKU) leads to chronic cerebral exposure to elevated phenylalanine (Phe) despite lifelong dietary treatment, yet the neurometabolic consequences in adulthood remain incompletely understood. Adults with early-treated PKU (n=30) and healthy controls (n=59) underwent proton magnetic resonance spectroscopy (1H-MRS), structural and functional brain MRI to evaluate white matter lesion load (T2-weighted images) and cerebral blood flow (arterial spin labeling), as well as cognitive assessments. 摘要：Multiple sclerosis (MS) fatigue is clinically obvious but mechanistically elusive. 摘要：One of the earliest systematic descriptions of facioscapulohumeral muscular dystrophy (FSHD) involved a six-generation kindred tracing back to a single ancestor born in 1775 in Lancashire, England, who later emigrated to Utah in the 1850s. 摘要：The noncoding functions of protein-coding messenger RNAs (mRNAs) are largely ignored. Here, we demonstrate an unexpected noncoding role of FMR1 mRNA in human neurodevelopment. A loss of FMR1 and its protein product, fragile X messenger ribonucleoprotein (FMRP), leads to the neurodevelopmental disorder fragile X syndrome (FXS). Neurons derived from FXS patients display hyperexcitability and immature morphology, whereas FMRP knockout (FMRP-KO) neurons that retain FMR1 transcript expression do not. Multi-omics analyses reveal key metabolic differences between FXS and FMRP-KO neurons. Although both FXS and FMRP-KO neurons exhibit reduced mitochondrial respiration, only FXS neurons exhibit elevated glycolysis. We further discover that the FMR1 transcript riboregulates the levels of the glycolytic enzyme lactate dehydrogenase A (LDHA) by inhibiting the binding of LDHA mRNA to insulin-like growth factor 2 mRNA-binding protein 2 (IGF2BP2). Finally, the FMR1 transcript, independent of FMRP, rescues glycolytic and cellular deficits of FXS neurons. Together, these results suggest that FMR1 is a dual-function RNA in human neurodevelopment.
