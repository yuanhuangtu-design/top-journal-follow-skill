摘要：Autism spectrum disorder (ASD) is a highly heritable condition with diverse clinical presentations. Approximately 15% of the genetic susceptibility of ASD is imparted by de novo haploinsufficient mutations of major effect. We mapped enhancers of two high-confidence autism genes, CHD8 and SCN2A, and used CRISPR-based gene activation targeting enhancers (eCRISPR-A) in human pluripotent stem cell (hPSC)-derived excitatory neurons and cerebral forebrain organoids to correct the effects of haploinsufficiency, taking advantage of the presence of a wild-type allele of each gene and endogenous gene regulation. Here we show that eCRISPR-A induced a sustained increase in CHD8 and SCN2A expression specifically in mutant neurons and cortical organoids, with rescue of gene expression levels and mutation-associated phenotypes, including gene expression and physiology. These data support gene activation via targeting enhancers of haploinsufficient genes as a therapeutic intervention in ASD and other neurodevelopmental disorders.

摘要：Amyotrophic lateral sclerosis/parkinsonism-dementia complex (ALS/PDC) is a fatal neurodegenerative disorder that was once hyperendemic in the island of Guam (Mariana Islands, US) and a few other Pacific locales. Despite extensive investigations into its origins, the etiology of ALS/PDC remains unclear. ALS/PDC is, at the neuropathology level, characterized by tau-dominant multiple proteinopathy in brain and spinal cord. It was recently reported that Guam ALS/PDC brain extracts exhibit tau seeding activity in fluorescence resonance energy transfer (FRET)-based biosensor cells. To build upon those findings and explore the nature of tau seeds in ALS/PDC in more detail, we used an alanine mutational scanning (Ala scan) approach to determine the seeding profile of tau in nervous tissues of Guam ALS/PDC cases. First, we confirmed the detection of tau seeding activity in ALS/PDC brain samples in tau biosensor cells. Notably, we could also detect potent tau seeding activity in spinal cord. Subsequent Ala scan assays demonstrated that ALS/PDC tau displays an aggregate incorporation pattern that resembles that of chronic traumatic encephalopathy (CTE)-type tau. This result is consistent with recent electron cryo-microscopy studies of tau, which revealed that ALS/PDC tau filaments are predominantly of the CTE-type. The structural characteristics and seeding behaviour of ALS/PDC tau, as well as the regional distribution of tau pathology at post-mortem, suggest that ALS/PDC is a CTE-like tauopathy.

作者：Al-Khazali HM, Hakimzadeh Z, Melchior AG, Alpay B et al.
链接：PubMed · DOI
命中检索：神经科学期刊周候选
发表形式：期刊论文（研究设计待核对）
摘要：Adrenomedullin (ADM), a vasoactive member of the calcitonin peptide family with receptor-signalling overlap with calcitonin gene-related peptide (CGRP), has emerged as a candidate therapeutic target in migraine. Given the striking clinical parallels between migraine and persistent post-traumatic headache (PTH), we investigated whether ADM-responsive signalling contributes to migraine-like headache in adults with persistent PTH attributed to mild traumatic brain injury. We conducted a single-centre, randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled, two-way crossover study in adults with persistent PTH and no pre-trauma history of migraine. On two experimental days separated by at least 7 days, participants received 20-minute intravenous infusions of ADM (19.9 pmol kg⁻¹ min⁻¹) and isotonic saline as the matched inactive comparator (placebo) in randomised sequence. Headache outcomes were recorded for 12 hours. The primary outcome was the within-participant incidence of experimentally induced migraine-like headache. The secondary outcome was the within-participant difference in baseline-corrected area under the curve (AUC) for headache intensity from 0 to 12 hours. Of 158 individuals assessed for eligibility, 21 were randomised, completed both periods and were analysed. Participants had a mean age of 34.3 years, 19 (90%) were female, and 18 (86%) reported a migraine-like phenotype. Migraine-like headache occurred in 17 of 21 participants (81%) after ADM and four of 21 (19%) following placebo. Fourteen participants developed migraine-like only after ADM and one only following placebo, yielding a paired absolute risk difference of 61.9 percentage points (95% CI 29.7-80.6; one-sided P < 0.001; two-sided P = 0.001). Baseline-corrected headache-intensity AUC was higher after ADM than placebo (median 1,110 [IQR 315-2205] versus 420 [-160 to 565]; P = 0.006). Median time to onset of migraine-like headache was 30 minutes after ADM and 390 minutes following placebo. Fifteen participants reported that the ADM-induced headache resembled their usual migraine-like headache episodes, and rescue medication was used by 10 participants after ADM and two after placebo. These findings identify pronounced responsiveness to ADM-linked signalling in persistent PTH and support the ADM pathway as a candidate target for mechanism-based therapeutic development, pending confirmation in independent cohorts and complementary preclinical studies.

Mosaic CDKL5 deficiency drives progressive inhibitory circuit remodelling.推荐
PMID 43021437 · Brain : a journal of neurology · 2026 11 11
作者：Mottolese N, Trazzi S, Medici G, Iannibelli F et al.
链接：PubMed · DOI
命中检索：神经科学期刊周候选
发表形式：期刊论文（研究设计待核对）
摘要：CDKL5 deficiency disorder predominantly affects heterozygous females, in whom random X-chromosome inactivation generates cellular CDKL5 mosaicism. Although mosaicism is a defining feature of the disease, whether it actively drives disease progression or simply reflects the genetic mode of inheritance remains unknown. Here, we combined age-comparative electrophysiology, quantitative synaptic imaging, transcriptomic profiling and postmortem human brain analyses to determine how mosaic CDKL5 deficiency shapes inhibitory circuit remodeling during disease progression. Heterozygous Cdkl5 female mice exhibited striking age- and region-specific remodeling of inhibitory neurotransmission. Hippocampal inhibition was initially enhanced but progressively declined with age, whereas cortical inhibitory transmission remained preserved at 3 months and increased in middle-aged animals. These functional changes were accompanied by dynamic remodeling of inhibitory presynaptic terminals, persistent disruption of inhibitory synapse organization and reduced hippocampal gephyrin-positive puncta despite preservation of the major inhibitory interneuron populations. At 12 months, heterozygous females also exhibited a higher density of IBA1/CD68 double-positive microglia in both regions compared with age-matched wild-type controls. Importantly, complete Cdkl5 deficiency failed to reproduce the progressive loss of inhibitory presynaptic terminals observed in heterozygous females, indicating that this phenotype is specific to the mosaic condition rather than a simple consequence of complete Cdkl5 loss. Transcriptomic profiling revealed a temporal transition from alterations detected at 3 months in immune-synaptic regulatory pathways to activity-dependent transcriptional programs associated with reduced structural and homeostatic support, consistent with progressive hippocampal circuit reorganization. Exploratory analyses of postmortem cortical tissue from individuals with CDD revealed differences in inhibitory presynaptic labeling between the available young and adult cases, suggesting that inhibitory alterations may also vary with age in human CDD. Together, these findings identify mosaic CDKL5 deficiency as a biologically distinct circuit state characterized by progressive, region-specific inhibitory circuit remodeling and provide a mechanistic framework for disease progression in CDKL5 deficiency disorder.
Neuronal cell-cycle re-entry stratifies clinical trajectories in amyotrophic lateral sclerosis.浏览
PMID 43021436 · Brain : a journal of neurology · 2026 11 11
作者：Plessis-Belair J, Sher RB
链接：PubMed · DOI
命中检索：神经科学期刊周候选
发表形式：期刊论文（研究设计待核对）
摘要：The clinical heterogeneity of Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis (ALS) remains a fundamental barrier to effective therapy. While cell cycle re-entry in post-mitotic neurons is a known precursor to neurodegeneration, its role in defining patient-specific outcomes has never been established. Here, we integrate large-scale transcriptomic profiling from the AnswerALS cohort with mechanistic studies in human iPSC-derived spinal motor neurons to define how cell-cycle re-entry shapes disease heterogeneity in ALS. We identify a transcriptionally distinct patient subgroup with the highest expression of G1 or G2-phase cyclins that exhibit significantly slower functional decline (ALSFRS-R), whereas the engagement of S-phase markers correlates with accelerated disease progression. Nucleocytoplasmic transport dysfunction, a hallmark of ALS and related disorders, disrupts the spatial distribution of key regulatory proteins and may contribute to maladaptive cell-cycle activation. Our recent evidence suggests that impaired nuclear import may initiate, rather than merely accompany, neuronal cell-cycle re-entry. Using iPSC-derived motor neurons, we show that impaired nuclear import initiates TDP-43 pathology and global nuclear/cytoplasmic mis-localization of key ALS-related proteins. This nucleocytoplasmic transport dysfunction triggers a transient wave of cell-cycle activity immediately preceding neuronal cell death. Crucially, we show that DNA-replication initiation is a pathological inflection point of no return, as neurons entering S-phase accumulate replication-associated DNA damage and undergo cell death. Selective inhibition of CDK4/6 prevents S-phase entry and confers potent neuroprotection. Our findings demonstrate that impaired nuclear import drives maladaptive cell-cycle reactivation in ALS and that the depth of cell-cycle engagement stratifies neuronal fate and patient trajectories.

摘要：Moyamoya angiopathy (MMA) is a rare cerebrovascular disorder characterized by progressive stenosis of intracranial arteries and the development of abnormal collateral vessels. While RNF213 is a major susceptibility gene in East Asian populations, the genetic architecture of MMA beyond RNF213, particularly in Europeans, remains largely undefined beyond a few known syndromic causes. We performed whole-exome sequencing on 74 sporadic MMA trios (proband and unaffected parents) of predominantly European ancestry. An additional cohort of 200 probands was screened for gene recurrence. We applied a stringent filtering strategy to identify de novo and rare inherited variants, followed by gene prioritization based on structural modeling, expression data, and biological plausibility. Gene-based burden tests were conducted against large control databases (gnomAD). We identified causative variants in 20 of the 74 probands (27%). Six probands with severe syndromic infant-onset phenotypes harbored de novo variants in the E3 ligase domain of RNF213. Fourteen probands carried a causative copy number variant (n = 3) or a short variant (n = 11) in a known MMA-associated gene (CBL, NF1, PTPN11, DIAPH1, ANO1, JAG1, NOS3, HBB). Beyond these established genes, we identified strong candidate variants in 11 probands from the discovery cohort, involving 11 novel candidate genes. Screening the additional cohort of 200 unrelated MMA probands identified recurrence of the gain-of-function SMAD4 p.Arg496Cys variant, which was de novo in the discovery-cohort proband, linking MMA to dysregulated TGF-β signaling. Other candidate findings included de novo loss-of-function variants in genes regulating epigenetic modulation and RNA splicing (SETD2, HNRNPL, UBAP2L, TOP2B, PRPF40A) and variants affecting vascular stress response (EPAS1, SVEP1, PDCD4). RNF213 was the only gene reaching exome-wide significance in burden testing (P = 2.11 × 10⁻11). This study establishes that MMA in non-East Asian populations is characterized by a strong genetic heterogeneity. These findings expand the molecular landscape of MMA and highlight the utility of trio-based sequencing for uncovering very rare molecular players in this disease. Additional analyses will now be needed to investigate the convergence of MMA pathways.

链接：PubMed · DOI

摘要：Chronic pain is highly prevalent worldwide, yet its genetic architecture remains to be fully elucidated. Here we leveraged data from 1,235,695 European-ancestry individuals across three biobanks. In a meta-analytic genome-wide association study of multidimensional pain (MP), we identified 343 independent loci, 92 of which were new pain associations. Sex-specific meta-analyses revealed 115 independent loci (12 of which were new) among males (n = 583,066) and 12 loci (2 of which were new) among females (n = 241,266). Multi-omic gene prioritization analyses highlighted 490 genes associated with MP through their effects on brain-specific and blood-specific regulation. This study expands our knowledge of the genetics of pain and its pathogenesis, highlighting the importance of its pleiotropy with multiple disorders and elucidating its multi-omic pathophysiology.

摘要：Sneezing, coughing, and pain are common symptoms of respiratory viral infection. Here, we show that the coronavirus papain-like protease (PLpro) directly activates airway-innervating nociceptors in mice and human induced pluripotent stem cell (iPSC)-derived nociceptors. 摘要：The spinal cord contains cell types critical for motor, sensory, and autonomic function, many of which are implicated in neurological disease and injury. Yet no shared molecular framework links human spinal cord cell types to the animal models used to study them. Here, we present a cross-species atlas integrating single-nucleus gene expression, chromatin accessibility, and spatial transcriptomics from over one million nuclei spanning cervical to sacral segments in human, macaque, and mouse. Consensus clustering resolves 111 cell type groups replicable across all three species, whose spatial distributions map spinal laminae and nuclei, including Clarke's column. 链接：PubMed · DOI
命中检索：神经科学期刊周候选

摘要：The full contribution of apolipoprotein E (APOE) to Alzheimer's disease burden in populations may have been overlooked. This is due, in large part, to a lack of recognition that the most common allele of the APOE gene, ε3, increases risk of Alzheimer's disease relative to the ε2 allele. When the impact of both ε3 and ε4 alleles of APOE are considered, the majority of late-onset Alzheimer's disease appears to be attributable to differences in APOE function or expression in populations of European ancestry and in other ethnic groups for which we have reliable data. Moreover, most sporadic early-onset Alzheimer's disease might not occur without the contributions of APOE ε3 and ε4, and the two alleles may be component causes of most forms of Alzheimer's disease arising from highly penetrant genetic mutations and trisomy 21. Taken together, these lines of epidemiological evidence suggest that APOE is a predominant cause of Alzheimer's disease, spanning the aetiologies of many forms of the disease. Consequently, almost all prospective cases of Alzheimer's disease might benefit from the development of therapies to modulate APOE's role in the disease's pathophysiology.

摘要：Behavioural variant frontotemporal dementia (bvFTD) can present with behavioural symptoms similar to psychiatric mimics of the disease (sometimes termed 'phenocopies'). In this prospective cohort study, 27 patients with frontotemporal lobar degeneration (FTLD) presenting as the bvFTD phenotype and 25 false-positives of bvFTD who ultimately had a psychiatric disorder underwent patient, informant, and clinician symptom ratings on a modified Frontal Behavioural Inventory (FBI). Formal quantification of discrepancy patterns in behaviour may reduce misdiagnosis in bvFTD. The neuroimaging findings support salience and semantic appraisal networks as the neuroanatomical basis for insight in bvFTD. Future studies should validate the FBI clinician version and examine factors underlying over-reporting by false-positives and their informants.

摘要：The rise and fall of a motivational state can unfold over many days. We used mouse mating behavior to model how the brain orchestrates such slow-timescale changes in motivation. Male mice become sexually satiated after successful matings, and their motivation to mate recovers over a week. Using deep-brain fluorescence-lifetime imaging in the medial preoptic area (MPOA), we found that tonic dopamine transmission-which regulates mating drive-also declined after mating and gradually re-emerged across several days. Two mechanisms regulated dopamine transmission. Second, these neurons' ability to produce and release dopamine dropped after mating and recovered over the following week, paralleling the recovery of mating drive. Therefore, changes in neuronal firing rate and neurotransmitter composition cooperate to control the early and late phases of motivational dynamics, respectively.

摘要：Sensory feedback can trigger rapid motor corrections, but how self-generated movement errors are corrected and influence subsequent behavior remains less clear. Here, we found that corrective submovements (CSMs), structured, feedback-driven adjustments, occurred either spontaneously or in response to neural perturbations during mouse reaching. Corrections were modestly encoded in the cerebellar anterior interposed nucleus (IntA) along with non-corrective reach kinematic correlates.
