摘要：BACKGROUND: Poststroke spasticity significantly impairs recovery, yet preventive strategies remain limited. METHODS: In this double-blind, randomized, sham-controlled trial, 70 patients with early subacute stroke (7-30 days) were randomized to receive 3 sessions of low-frequency (1 Hz, 1000 pulses at 90% resting motor threshold) rTMS over contralesional primary motor cortex (n=35) or sham stimulation (n=35), alongside standard care. The primary outcome was upper-limb spasticity assessed using the modified Modified Ashworth Scale. Secondary outcomes included handgrip strength, muscle power assessed by the Medical Research Council scale, pain assessed by the Visual Analogue Scale, modified Rankin Scale, and Stroke Impact Scale 3.0. RESULTS: rTMS therapy significantly lowered spasticity scores at 1 and 3 months across all assessed upper-limb joints (P=0.001), improved handgrip strength (P=0.01), and prevented pain (P=0.001). Stroke Impact Scale scores at 1 month showed greater gains in activities of daily living, mobility, hand function, social participation, and recovery in the rTMS group (P=0.001). Functional independence (modified Rankin Scale) was significantly better in the rTMS group (P=0.006) at 3 months. On binary logistic regression, sham stimulation was associated with greater odds of developing spasticity at 1 month (shoulder odds ratio [OR], 8.29 [95% CI, 1.95-35.27], P=0.04; elbow OR, 7.62 [95% CI, 1.69-34.35], P=0.08; wrist OR, 5.18 [95% CI, 1.60-16.78], P=0.06). At 3 months, sham stimulation remained predictive, with higher odds of spasticity at the shoulder (OR, 15.3 [95% CI, 8.26-28.43]), elbow (OR, 13.43 [95% CI, 3.94-45.78]), wrist (OR, 19.9 [95% CI, 4.9-80.96]), and fingers (OR, 26.25 [95% CI, 5.19-132.87], all P=0.001). Minor adverse events like headache and light-headedness were observed in 5 patients. CONCLUSIONS: Low-frequency rTMS over contralesional primary motor cortex significantly prevented spasticity, improved motor recovery, reduced pain, improved functional outcomes and quality of life in early subacute stroke, supporting its role as a promising adjunctive therapy in early stroke rehabilitation. 摘要：BACKGROUND AND OBJECTIVES: Individuals with epilepsy face an elevated risk of suicide, but risk factors for suicide remain incompletely understood. We conducted a nationwide case-control study in Sweden to investigate clinical characteristics associated with suicide risk in persons with epilepsy. METHODS: Persons with epilepsy were identified through Swedish nationwide registers, with epilepsy diagnoses validated through medical record review. Cases were individuals who died by suicide between 2006 and 2011, validated through autopsy reports, and controls were derived from the source population using similar validation procedures. Characteristics included epilepsy type, etiology, tonic-clonic seizure (TCS) frequency, antiseizure medication (ASM) treatment, living conditions, and psychiatric comorbidity. Odds ratios (ORs) with 95% CIs were estimated using conditional logistic regression. RESULTS: The study included 111 suicide cases and 1,147 controls. Suicide cases had a mean age at epilepsy onset of 20 years, and 35% were female. Alcohol abuse (OR 11.81, 95% CI 6.25-22.30) and other substance abuse (OR 10.71, 95% CI 4.52-25.38) showed stronger associations than depression (OR 2.94, 95% CI 1.56-5.53) and anxiety disorders (OR 2.93, 95% CI 1.57-5.44). Structural and unknown epilepsy etiologies were associated with elevated risk (OR 3.07, 95% CI 1.0-9.5 and OR 8.80, 95% CI 2.9-27.1, respectively). ASM treatment was associated with lower suicide risk (monotherapy OR 0.51, 95% CI 0.27-0.95; polytherapy OR 0.21, 95% CI 0.1-0.5), and nonadherence was associated with higher risk (OR 3.02, 95% CI 1.8-5.1). DISCUSSION: This study identified a distinct risk profile for suicide in persons with epilepsy, highlighting alcohol and substance abuse, living alone, seizure burden, nonadherence, and structural or unknown etiologies as key risk factors. The stronger associations for substance abuse may reflect characteristics specific to epilepsy populations and the focus on completed suicide. Screening for substance abuse and improved seizure control may contribute to suicide prevention. Major limitations include the modest number of cases and incomplete information on prior suicidal behavior.

摘要：Voltage-gated sodium channels regulate neuronal excitability and synaptic transmission in the postnatal and adult brain. The gene SCN2A, encoding the sodium channel Nav1.2, regulates synaptic development and variants in SCN2A are associated with autism spectrum disorders (ASD) and a broad spectrum of epilepsy phenotypes, including early-onset developmental and epileptic encephalopathies. The expression pattern of SCN2A begins during prenatal cortical development, prior to the onset of synaptic transmission, but it is unknown whether SCN2A regulates early cortical development through mechanisms independent of synaptic transmission. METHODS: We performed a retrospective study on 21,072 children evaluated for epilepsy in two Child Neurology referral centres in Eastern Sicily (i.e. Messina, Catania, and Enna) between January 2001 and December 2025. RESULTS: Fifty-seven children (0.27%) had a co-diagnosis of epilepsy and IEM (28 males, 29 females). 摘要：OBJECTIVE: To systematically review evidence describing the use of ketogenic diet therapy in adults with status epilepticus. METHODS: A PRISMA-compliant systematic review was conducted and registered in PROSPERO. MEDLINE, EMBASE, CINAHL, and Cochrane CENTRAL were searched from inception to June 13, 2025. Eligible studies included original research involving adults 18 years and older with status epilepticus treated with enteral or parenteral ketogenic diet therapy. Outcomes included ketosis, seizure control, mortality, functional outcomes, adverse events and ICU/hospital outcomes. Study quality was assessed using design appropriate tools. RESULTS: Twenty-three studies comprising 123 adult patients were included, primarily of observational studies, case series, and case reports. Ketosis and seizure cessation were frequently reported, occurring in 79 % and 81 % of patients respectively. Reported median or individual times from ketogenic diet initiation and ketosis to seizure cessation ranged from two to 25 days and zero to 13.5 days respectively. Mortality among cohorts with deaths varied widely, overlapping with historically reported mortality for super-refractory status epilepticus. Heterogeneity in study design, patient populations, diet protocols, ketosis monitoring, and outcome definitions precluded meta-analysis. CONCLUSION: Evidence suggests an association between KD therapy and seizure cessation in adults with refractory and super-refractory status epilepticus, although evidence remains limited.
