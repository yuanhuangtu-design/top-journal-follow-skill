摘要：BACKGROUND AND OBJECTIVES: Genetic testing is increasingly used in presurgical evaluation, but the yield of resection and ablation across germline genetic epilepsies remains uncertain. METHODS: We conducted a systematic review of MEDLINE (PubMed) and Scopus and added cases from 3 institutional cohorts and the Pediatric Epilepsy Research Consortium (PERC) databases, including individuals with pathogenic/likely pathogenic germline variants besides tuberous sclerosis who underwent resection or laser ablation. Etiologies were grouped into biologically informed categories (GATORopathies, vascular, overgrowth, copy number variants (CNVs), channelopathies, synaptopathies, and other). Primary outcome was seizure freedom (Engel I) at the last follow-up. Group comparisons used Fisher exact and Kruskal-Wallis tests (α = 0.05; Bonferroni when applicable). Prespecified sensitivity analyses stratified by lesional status, excluded GATORopathies, and restricted to literature-only cases. RESULTS: We included 224 literature cases (65 studies), 36 institutional cases, and 12 PERC cases (n = 272). The median follow-up was 24 months (interquartile range 12-48.0). Seizure freedom was achieved in vascular disorders 14/19 (73.6%), GATORopathies 79/120 (65.8%), CNVs 18/31 (58.0%), overgrowth 9/16 (56.6%), channelopathies 13/43 (30.0%), synaptopathies 4/18 (22.2%), and other 11/25 (44.0%) (overall p < 0.001). Among cases with available imaging results, 208/253 (82.2%) had epileptogenic MRI lesions, including 64% of channelopathies and 80% of the synaptopathies. In univariate contrasts (each category vs all others), odds of seizure freedom were higher for vascular disorders (2.64-fold, 95% CI 0.87-9.65) and GATORopathies (2.41-fold, 95% CI 1.44-4.09), and lower for synaptopathies (∼4.2-fold lower, OR 0.23, 95% CI 0.05-0.77) and channelopathies (∼4.5-fold lower, OR 0.22, 95% CI 0.09-0.48). Direction and magnitude were consistent across prespecified sensitivity analyses (lesional only, literature only, exclusion of GATORopathies). DISCUSSION: Resective surgeries can be effective in germline genetic epilepsies, but outcomes vary by pathway. Disorders with discrete, lesional substrates (GATORopathies, vascular) show the highest likelihood of seizure freedom, whereas channelopathies and synaptopathies, despite the presence of MRI lesions, have substantially lower yields even in lesional cases. Prospective, genotype-aware surgical registries with standardized reporting (EEG, imaging, and pathology) and time-to-event outcomes are needed to refine the selection of surgical candidates and quantify seizure and non-seizure-related outcomes.

摘要：IMPORTANCE: Despite the profound impact of neonatal and infantile-onset epilepsies on neurodevelopmental outcomes and mortality, consolidated global epidemiological evidence remains lacking. OBJECTIVES: To estimate the global incidence and prevalence of neonatal and infantile-onset epilepsies and epilepsy syndromes and to characterize their associated etiological profiles. DATA SOURCES: PubMed, Embase, Web of Science, and Scopus were searched from inception through December 18, 2025. STUDY SELECTION: Population-based observational studies reporting the incidence or prevalence of neonatal and infantile-onset epilepsies or epilepsy syndromes were eligible for inclusion. DATA EXTRACTION AND SYNTHESIS: Two authors independently and in duplicate performed study selection, data extraction, and quality assessment of included studies using the Joanna Briggs Institute Critical Appraisal checklist for prevalence studies. Generalized linear mixed-model random-effects meta-analysis was used to pool the estimates of incidence, prevalence, and etiology. Subgroup meta-analyses were conducted by country, World Health Organization (WHO) region, World Bank income, and diagnostic criteria. Metaregression was used to investigate the association between study-level covariates and pooled incidence estimates of infantile-onset epilepsy syndromes. MAIN OUTCOMES AND MEASURES: Cumulative incidence and prevalence of neonatal and infantile-onset epilepsies and epilepsy syndromes. RESULTS: A total of 86 population-based studies from 30 countries across 4 WHO regions were included. The pooled cumulative incidence of infantile-onset epilepsies within the first 2 years of life was 236.0 cases (95% CI, 164.7-338.2; 95% prediction interval [PI], 53.0-1044.3) per 100 000 live births. Among epilepsy syndrome subgroups, developmental and epileptic encephalopathies (DEEs) had the highest incidence at 67.7 cases (95% CI, 42.1-108.9; 95% PI, 7.1-636.1) per 100 000 live births. Infantile epileptic spasms syndrome (IESS) was the most incident DEE, with 31.6 cases (95% CI, 27.5-36.2; 95% PI, 13.7-72.6) per 100 000 live births. Among self-limited epilepsies, self-limited infantile epilepsy had the highest incidence at 13.0 cases (95% CI, 9.2-18.2; 95% PI, 6.4-26.4) per 100 000 live births. Subgroup analyses revealed limited geographical representativeness, with the majority of studies from high-income countries in the European Region. CONCLUSIONS AND RELEVANCE: The findings underscore a significant global burden of neonatal and infantile-onset epilepsies, primarily driven by DEEs, specifically IESS. Given substantial between-study heterogeneity, the pooled estimates should be interpreted as broad indicators rather than precise, generalizable rates. Geographic disparities, with a lack of data from the African and Eastern Mediterranean Regions, underscore the need for population-based studies in underrepresented regions and longitudinal research on survival, remission, and functional outcomes.

摘要：AIM: To quantify long-term trends in Global Burden of Disease (GBD) idiopathic epilepsy (genetic/unknown-cause epilepsy) across European countries from 1990-2023, examining overall burden, disability, and burden from premature mortality. METHODS: We analyzed age-standardized Disability-Adjusted Life Years (DALY), Years of Life Lost (YLL), and Years Lived with Disability (YLD) in 43 European countries using GBD 2023 data. Country-specific Estimated Annual Percentage Changes (EAPCs) summarized average long-term trends from 1990 to 2023, and longitudinal models estimated the average European trend. Additional analyses tested whether the main findings were sensitive to the shape of the time trend, correlation between nearby years, or the influence of individual and small countries. RESULTS: The burden declined on average across Europe, but national trajectories differed substantially. The estimated average annual changes were -0.59% for DALY (95% CI -0.89 to -0.28), -0.60% for YLL (-1.08 to -0.13), and -0.54% for YLD (-0.73 to -0.35). Some of the largest reductions occurred in selected Eastern European and former Soviet countries, particularly for YLL, but this pattern was not uniform across Eastern Europe. Declines were greater in males than females for all three burden measures. The overall European decline remained consistent across the additional analyses. CONCLUSIONS: Age-standardized DALY, YLL, and YLD rates for GBD idiopathic epilepsy declined on average across 43 European countries from 1990 to 2023. This overall improvement masked major national differences, particularly in burden from premature mortality. Country-specific monitoring remains important because the European average does not describe all national trajectories.

摘要：BACKGROUND: To determine seizure recurrence rates and independent risk factors following antiseizure medication (ASM) withdrawal in pediatric epilepsy, and integrate these findings into a personalized web-based clinical decision support system (CDSS). METHODS: In this prospective cohort study, 318 pediatric epilepsy patients with ≥24 months of clinical seizure freedom and completely normal pre-withdrawal EEGs were evaluated during ASM withdrawal. Independent risk factors were identified using a multivariable Cox proportional hazards model and embedded into a dynamic web tool. RESULTS: Seizure recurrence occurred in 25.8% (n = 82) of patients (mean time: 4.70 ± 13.96 months). The Cox model demonstrated acceptable discrimination (C-index = 0.808, optimism-corrected C-index = 0.769). Independent predictors of recurrence were status epilepticus (SE; HR = 2.718, 95% CI: 1.193-6.194; p = 0.017), febrile seizures (FS; HR = 2.300, 95% CI: 1.214-4.356; p = 0.011), and active epilepsy duration (HR = 1.022 per month, 95% CI: 1.016-1.027; p < 0.001). Compared to ≤24 months, pre-withdrawal seizure freedom of 25-35 months (HR = 0.231, p = 0.001) and ≥36 months (HR = 0.232, p = 0.002) both conferred an approximate 77% risk reduction. Mandatory pre-withdrawal EEG normalization served as a controlled methodological feature isolating clinical predictors in EEG-normal children. Among 35 patients undergoing a second withdrawal attempt, 11 (31.4%) experienced recurrence. CONCLUSIONS: SE, FS, active epilepsy duration, and pre-withdrawal seizure-free duration independently predict recurrence in children with normal pre-withdrawal EEGs. Embedding this model into a web tool enables dynamic, evidence-based risk stratification in pediatric practice.

摘要：OBJECTIVE: Seizure unpredictability remains a major contributor to morbidity in epilepsy, and biomarkers capable of identifying pre-ictal states are critically needed. Volatile organic compounds (VOCs) have emerged as potential noninvasive indicators of seizure susceptibility, yet the biological mechanisms linking pre-ictal neural states to peripheral volatile signatures remain poorly understood. METHODS: We conducted a narrative review in accordance with the Scale for the Assessment of Narrative Review Articles (SANRA) criteria integrating evidence from epilepsy neurobiology, oxidative stress, autonomic physiology, volatilomics, and seizure-detection science to develop a mechanistic hypothesis linking pre-ictal physiological processes to seizure-associated volatile signatures. RESULTS: Evidence supports several components of the proposed mechanistic hypothesis, although the strength and directness of evidence vary across pathway components. Pre-ictal states have been associated with increased neuronal excitability, altered neurophysiology, and heightened metabolic demand, creating conditions favorable for oxidative stress. Lipid peroxidation generates volatile and semi-volatile metabolites that can enter systemic circulation and become detectable peripherally. Autonomic modulation may further influence their expression and detectability. Human olfactory and analytical studies provide preliminary evidence for seizure-associated and pre-ictal volatile signatures. Although the complete pathway has not been experimentally validated, available evidence is consistent with a biologically plausible hypothesis linking central physiological processes to peripheral volatile signals. SIGNIFICANCE: This review proposes a multilevel mechanistic hypothesis for seizure-associated volatile signature generation.
