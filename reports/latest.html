Knowledge, self-management, and health-related quality of life among Omani adults with generalized epilepsy: A cross-sectional study.必读

作者：Al-Hajri S, Lazarus ER, Omari OA, Zaabi OA et al.
链接：PubMed · DOI
命中检索：癫痫专科刊 × Epilepsy
摘要：PURPOSE: To assess epilepsy-related knowledge, self-management (SM) practices, and health-related quality of life (HR-QoL) among Omani adults with generalized epilepsy and to examine the correlations between these variables. METHODS: A descriptive cross-sectional study was conducted among 200 Omani adults with epilepsy attending three tertiary hospitals in Muscat. Knowledge was measured using the Epilepsy Knowledge Scale-19, SM practices using the Epilepsy Self-Management Scale, and HR-QoL using the Quality of Life in Epilepsy-31. Data were analyzed using descriptive statistics and inferential statistics with a significance level of p ≤ 0.05. RESULTS: Slightly more than half of the participants were male (52.5%), with ages ranging from 18 to 75 years (M = 33.29). Overall, participants demonstrated poor knowledge (M = 7.81/19), acceptable SM skills (M = 131.60/190), and low HR-QoL (M = 56.55/100). A significant positive correlation was found between knowledge and HR-QoL (r = 0.261, p < 0.001), whereas the correlations between knowledge and SM skills and between SM practices and HR-QoL were not significant (p = 0.710 and p = 0.104, respectively). Female gender, unemployment, presence of comorbidities, older age (≥ 50 years), and more frequent recent seizures were all significantly associated with lower HR-QoL (p ≤ 0.05). CONCLUSION: Omani adults with epilepsy exhibit poor knowledge, only acceptable self-management skills, and low HR-QoL. The positive association between knowledge and HR-QoL highlights the need to strengthen epilepsy-related education. Culturally tailored educational and self-management support for patients, families, and the wider public is required to improve knowledge, optimize self-management, and enhance HR-QoL in Oman.
Cannabidiol in pediatric drug-resistant epilepsies: A systematic review and meta-analysis of seizure responder rates and neurobehavioral outcome reporting.必读
PMID 42914761 · Epilepsy research · 2026 Oct 15
作者：Praticò AD
链接：PubMed · DOI
命中检索：癫痫专科刊 × Epilepsy
摘要：BACKGROUND: Cannabidiol (CBD) has emerged as an important adjunctive treatment option for pediatric drug-resistant epilepsies (DREs), particularly in developmental and epileptic encephalopathies (DEEs) such as Dravet syndrome and Lennox-Gastaut syndrome. However, the current literature remains heterogeneous and predominantly focused on seizure outcomes, with limited evaluation of broader neurodevelopmental domains. METHODS: A systematic review with meta-analysis of proportions was conducted according to PRISMA 2020 recommendations. PubMed/MEDLINE was searched for studies evaluating CBD treatment in pediatric epilepsies (search updated to October 1st 2026), supplemented by citation searching. Randomized controlled trials and observational studies were included; secondary reports of the same cohort (extensions, post hoc and sub-analyses) were linked to their parent study. Extracted variables included study design, epilepsy syndrome, follow-up duration, seizure outcomes, cognitive outcomes, and behavioral outcomes. The primary outcome was the proportion of patients achieving ≥ 50% seizure reduction at the earliest stable post-titration timepoint, pooled across all studies with ≥ 10 assessed patients using a random-effects model (logit transformation, DerSimonian-Laird estimator). RESULTS: Sixty-three studies (92 reports) were included, predominantly observational (56/63, 88.9%). The meta-analysis included 37 studies (3439 assessed patients; 1610 responders) and yielded a pooled ≥ 50% responder rate of 48.4% (95% CI 44.5-52.3), with substantial heterogeneity (I²=76.4%; 95% prediction interval 29.4-67.8%). Responder rates were lower in the CBD arms of randomized trials (41.3%) than in observational studies (49.8%), and higher at long-term than at short-term assessments (57.4% vs 42.8%). Seizure outcomes were reported in 98.4% of included studies, whereas cognitive and behavioral outcomes were reported in only 7.9% and 27.0% of studies, respectively. CONCLUSIONS: Approximately half of children with drug-resistant epilepsies treated with adjunctive cannabidiol achieve a ≥ 50% seizure reduction in the available, predominantly observational literature; randomized trials suggest a somewhat lower proportion, and long-term estimates are likely inflated by selective retention. The literature remains strongly seizure-centered, while cognitive and behavioral outcomes are inconsistently assessed and poorly standardized. Future pediatric CBD studies should incorporate harmonized longitudinal follow-up and standardized neurodevelopmental outcome measures alongside seizure outcomes.
Addressing stigma in epilepsy care: a systematic review of personalized anti-stigma interventions.必读
PMID 42914581 · Seizure · 2026 Oct 15
作者：Ghorbannezhad G, Hassanabadi R, Gorji A
链接：PubMed · DOI
命中检索：癫痫专科刊 × Epilepsy
摘要：Epilepsy-related stigma (ERS) continues to be a significant challenge affecting people with epilepsy (PWE) worldwide and is closely associated with reduced quality of life and poor treatment adherence. Although various anti-ERS interventions have been developed, the effectiveness of personalized approaches has not been systematically evaluated. This systematic review synthesizes the available evidence on personalized anti-ERS interventions in epilepsy, focusing on their effectiveness as a strategy to reduce ERS. We systematically searched for studies evaluating personalized interventions aimed at ERS, including internalized, perceived, enacted, and courtesy stigma, and examined their effects on related psychosocial outcomes. We coded interventions across four personalization domains: age, culture, clinical/condition-specific factors, and setting. Of the 1,454 records screened, 74 studies from 34 countries were included, comprising 45 studies that directly evaluated the effectiveness of personalized anti-ERS interventions and 29 that indirectly assessed their potential impact on ERS through related psychological or quality of life outcomes. Overall, 78.4% (58/74) of interventions reported favorable outcomes, with favorable outcomes reported for 94.3% (33/35) of highly personalized interventions, compared with 73.1% (19/26) of moderately personalized and 46.2% (6/13) of low/basic personalization interventions. Few interventions targeted adolescents and transitional-age youth, and most studies were conducted in high-income countries. This study suggests an association between greater personalization and more favorable intervention outcomes. Personalized, collaboratively developed, culturally relevant, and developmentally tailored strategies are needed, particularly for adolescents and in low- and middle-income countries, alongside future research integrating standardized outcomes, long-term follow-up, and structural approaches to achieve sustainable stigma reduction.
Impact of entry trajectory angle-to-skull on electrode placement accuracy in robot-assisted stereoelectroencephalography.推荐

作者：Park C, Kim JH, Kim JH, Shin JH et al.
链接：PubMed · DOI
命中检索：癫痫专科刊 × Epilepsy
摘要：OBJECTIVE: Despite the widespread use of robotic systems to improve stereotactic accuracy in stereoelectroencephalography (SEEG), targeting errors still occur. Several studies have suggested that the trajectory-to-skull angle is closely associated with targeting error among various influencing factors; however, quantitative analyses remain limited. Therefore, this study aimed to quantitatively evaluate the relationship between trajectory-to-skull angle and implantation accuracy and to establish practical cut-off values for surgical planning. METHODS: A total of 10 patients (103 electrodes) were included in this study and various clinical variables were analyzed to identify whether trajectory-to-skull angle has a significant relation with accuracy using multivariate linear regression. Then, receiver operating characteristic (ROC) curve analysis with Youden index optimization was applied to determine angle thresholds for achieving ≤ 3 mm accuracy. RESULTS: Multivariate analysis showed that both axial and coronal trajectory-to-skull angles were significantly associated with target error, with more tangential incidence angles corresponding to larger deviations (p < 0.01). Axial angle-to-skull was particularly associated with anteroposterior (Y-axis) error, whereas coronal angle-to-skull primarily predicted superoinferior (Z-axis) error. ROC curve analysis identified optimal angle thresholds of 11.8° for axial trajectories and 6.3° for coronal trajectories to predict targeting accuracy within 3 mm. When both axial and coronal angles were maintained within these cut-offs, the probability of achieving a target error ≤ 3 mm was 88.2%. CONCLUSION: Trajectory-to-skull angle is a key geometric determinant of electrode placement accuracy in robot-assisted SEEG. Quantitative angle thresholds identified in this study provide practical guidance for trajectory planning and may help minimize mechanical deviation, improve implantation safety, and enhance the reliability of seizure-network localization.

摘要：BACKGROUND: Epilepsy affects over 50 million people worldwide, and approximately one third of patients develop drug-resistant epilepsy (DRE), a major driver of morbidity, mortality, and healthcare costs. Vagus nerve stimulation (VNS) is an established neuromodulatory treatment option for DRE, but local guidance on its use in the United Arab Emirates (UAE) remains limited. This study aimed to establish an expert consensus to define best practices for the effective and efficient use of the VNS in the UAE setting. METHODS: A multidisciplinary Task Force conducted a literature review and developed 25 evidence- and experience-informed statements, which were refined during an in-person expert meeting to ensure clinical relevance for the UAE context. Statements were rated through a single-round modified Delphi process involving UAE neurologists and neurosurgeons. Participants rated their agreement with each statement using a 7-point Likert scale. Consensus was predefined as ≥ 70% agreement, corresponding to ratings of 6-7. Statements with 60-69% agreement were classified as moderate agreement but were not considered to have reached consensus. RESULTS: Fifty-six experts completed the Delphi round, corresponding to a 75.7% response rate. Item-level denominators varied across statements. Eighteen of 25 statements reached consensus, six demonstrated moderate agreement, and one did not reach consensus. Strong agreement was observed for patient selection, safety, structured parameter optimization, and multidisciplinary care pathways. Moderate agreement emerged for presurgical evaluation, timing of implantation, and selected clinical scenarios. CONCLUSION: Building on existing international guidance and previous Delphi work in other settings, this study provides a UAE-specific description of expert agreement and variability on the clinical and organizational use of VNS for DRE. The findings may inform future local pathway development and identify areas requiring clearer definitions, prospective data collection, and further evidence generation. PLAIN LANGUAGE SUMMARY: This study asked expert clinicians in the United Arab Emirates to rate 25 statements on the clinical and organizational use of vagus nerve stimulation for drug-resistant epilepsy. Vagus nerve stimulation is an adjunctive treatment that aims to reduce seizure burden and improve patient-centred outcomes; it should not be interpreted primarily as a treatment intended to achieve complete seizure freedom. Eighteen statements reached the prespecified consensus threshold, while six showed moderate agreement and one did not reach consensus. Experts showed agreement on several areas, including multidisciplinary care, safety considerations, MRI compatibility, structured follow-up, and individualized programming. However, agreement was more limited for some topics, including expected responder rates after one year, timing of implantation, selected programming criteria, and quality-of-life effects independent of seizure reduction. These findings identify areas where expert views are aligned and areas where practice remains variable. They may support the development of more consistent local care pathways, while highlighting topics that require further clinical evidence and local guidance.

摘要：OBJECTIVE: Cognitive impairment is cited as one of the most consequential comorbidities of epilepsy. Cognitive difficulties may be managed using compensatory strategies. The Everyday Compensation (EComp) Questionnaire offers a means of assessing the use of these strategies but has only been validated for neurologically healthy older adults. The present study examined the preliminary psychometric properties of the EComp Questionnaire in epilepsy. METHODS: 37 adults with focal epilepsy participating in a randomized controlled trial of a cognitive prehabilitation program completed the EComp Questionnaire, Quality of Life Epilepsy Inventory-31 (QOLIE-31), and the Instrumental Activities of Daily-Living Scale (IADL-C) at baseline and follow-up. 摘要：Migraine and epilepsy are paroxysmal neurological disorders that frequently co-occur and share common pathophysiological mechanisms. 摘要：Some patients with hyperkinetic movement disorders may present with a severe and prolonged state of uncontrolled chorea and/or ballism, termed 'choreic storm', causing distress and prompting intervention. These events may be more frequent and severe in patients with childhood-onset movement disorders. Deep brain stimulation (DBS) can be effective for medically refractory dyskinesias including chorea, and the availability of event-based local field potential (LFP) sensing in clinically available devices present an opportunity to better understand the underlying neurophysiology of these episodic phenomena. We analyzed clinical, electrophysiological, and corroborating video data from a patient with a de novo CEP85L mutation (NM_001042475.2, c.2381A>G, p.Y794C) presenting with chorea-predominant dyskinetic cerebral palsy, microcephaly, and intellectual disability. The patient underwent bilateral pallidal DBS for medically refractory symptoms and showed improvement in her baseline chorea. However, choreic storms continued multiple times per week, requiring frequent rescue medications and/or emergency medical intervention. These episodes were associated with autonomic features and variable triggers. Family-initiated LFP event recording supplemented by concurrent video facilitated side-by-side analysis of clinical episodes, revealing elevated LFP power in the theta to low gamma frequencies during choreic storms compared to baseline event recordings. Episode-associated increases in LFP power captured during choreic storms may represent a putative within-patient electrophysiological correlate of these severe episodic exacerbations. Although requiring confirmation and extension in larger series, these observations suggest that adaptive stimulation paradigms could be useful for patients experiencing choreic storms.
