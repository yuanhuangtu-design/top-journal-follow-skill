摘要：The discovery of antibody-mediated glial and neuronal disorders has transformed the diagnosis and treatment of neurological diseases previously considered idiopathic or not recognised as immune-mediated. Antibody-mediated disorders targeting glial aquaporin-4 and myelin oligodendrocyte glycoprotein are distinct nosological entities, defined by specific targets, mechanisms, syndromes, and therapeutic implications. By contrast, antibody-mediated disorders targeting neuronal antigens (ie, autoimmune encephalitides) are still frequently analysed collectively, with data from different disorders pooled as if they represent a single disease rather than distinct entities. Evidence from anti-NMDA receptor encephalitis, anti-LGI1 encephalitis, and anti-IgLON5 disease (used here as exemplars of autoimmune encephalitides) indicates that individual autoimmune encephalitides differ in demographics, triggers, mechanisms, clinical manifestations, post-acute trajectories, and treatment requirements. The broad, heterogeneous presentations of autoimmune encephalitides require coordinated multidisciplinary care within and beyond neurology, involving specialists in paediatrics, infectious diseases, epilepsy, movement disorders, sleep medicine, intensive care, cognitive rehabilitation, and other relevant disciplines. Such interdisciplinary collaboration facilitates the recognition of new diseases and elucidation of the causes of previously unclassified disorders. Improving patient care requires not only aetiological and mechanistic insight to enable development of targeted therapies but also strengthened cross-specialty expertise to optimise diagnosis and management.

摘要：Traumatic brain injury (TBI) and spinal cord injury (SCI) affect more than 220 million people worldwide and are associated with cardiometabolic, endocrine, neurological, and psychiatric sequelae for years to decades after an acute injury. However, most health systems still treat neurotrauma (TBI and SCI, collectively) as a time-limited event, leaving long-term surveillance of these patients fragmented or absent, which leads to preventable mortality and avoidable readmissions. TBI and SCI in adults should be reclassified as acute-onset multisystem chronic-risk conditions. Existing cardiovascular, endocrine, and psychiatric services do not capture neurotrauma-specific risks, including trauma-induced hypopituitarism, autonomic dysregulation, accelerated ageing, and cognitive-behavioural impairment, so neurotrauma care is best framed as a missing node in chronic neurological care. 摘要：BACKGROUND AND OBJECTIVES: Epilepsy is linked to late-onset dementia, but evidence for young-onset dementia (YOD; <65 years) is limited. We assessed the epilepsy-YOD association in France and Sweden and YOD risk after midlife epilepsy. METHODS: We conducted 3 nationwide analyses. A French nested case-control analysis included patients with incident YOD aged 40-64 years, diagnosed between January 1, 2016, and December 31, 2018. Index date was the first YOD-related health care event; controls were risk-set matched on age, sex, and region. A Swedish nested case-control replication included patients with YOD diagnosed between January 1, 1987, and December 31, 2016, with matched controls. In both studies, epilepsy-related diagnoses and antiseizure medication (ASM) dispensations were assessed up to 10 years before index date. Odds ratios (ORs) were estimated with conditional logistic regression. A French retrospective cohort included adults aged 30-55 years with incident epilepsy in 2008, matched to individuals with prevalent epilepsy before 2008 or no epilepsy. The cohort index was the first ASM reimbursement in the incident epilepsy group or the matched date for comparators. Follow-up ended at YOD, death, or 10 years. We estimated 10-year cumulative incidence and adjusted (comorbidities, deprivation) hazard ratios (HRs), treating death as a competing event. Sensitivity analyses included lagged entry up to 5 years. RESULTS: Case-control studies included 11,360 cases and 113,600 controls in France and 16,433 cases and 164,330 controls in Sweden. The French cohort included 12,901 individuals with incident epilepsy, 64,083 with prevalent epilepsy, and 129,010 without epilepsy (mean age 44.0 years; 55.5% female participants). Ten-year cumulative YOD incidence was 2.20% (95% CI 1.94-2.48) with incident epilepsy (adjusted HR compared with individuals without epilepsy 6.02, 95% CI 4.98-7.26), 1.74% (1.64-1.85) with prevalent epilepsy (adjusted HR 5.38, 95% CI 4.64-6.25), and 0.21% (0.19-0.24) without epilepsy. Associations attenuated with longer lags but persisted beyond 5 years. DISCUSSION: Across 2 countries and complementary designs, epilepsy was strongly associated with YOD. Epilepsy conferred higher YOD risk in midlife, especially with multimorbidity, supporting epilepsy inclusion in YOD risk stratification and prevention.

摘要：One-third of the world's 70 million people with epilepsy have seizures uncontrolled by medication, and implantable devices are a promising treatment. These devices can detect impending seizures, missed medication and impaired cognition, but, like most medical devices, they cannot share this information with patients in real time. This is a missed opportunity for devices to learn from clinical events and teach patients how their behaviors affect their health. Here we present a device platform that converses with patients to help them manage their epilepsy. The inpatient prototype links scalp and intracranial electroencephalography (EEG) to a secure, large language model-based artificial intelligence (AI) that communicates bidirectionally through a smartphone app. The AI processes biomarkers of sleep, medication, cognition and seizure probability, notifies patients of clinical events and trends, records symptoms and responses, and uses their feedback to retrain its seizure detector, reducing false alarms. We validated this platform in 13 patients who are undergoing video-EEG monitoring for epilepsy (scalp, n = 11; intracranial, n = 2). In a pseudoprospective simulation, seizure detectors improved precision from patient feedback alone, without expert intervention. We propose this system as a scalable, safeguarded platform for medical devices that can rapidly adapt to patient and provider needs across epilepsy and other medical conditions.

摘要：Emotional experiences during focal seizures are often misinterpreted as psychiatric disorders. We aimed to evaluate symptoms involving affective symptoms at seizure onset. We aimed to identify the most prevalent affective symptoms at seizure onset and characterize their localizing and lateralizing value. We performed a scoping review based on Preferred Reporting Items for Systematic reviews and Meta-Analyses-extension for Scoping Reviews guidelines. A systematic search identified records from 4 databases: PubMed, Embase, CINAHL (via EBSCOhost), and Web of Science. Two reviewers independently screened titles/abstracts and full texts; discrepancies were resolved by consensus. This review focused on patients with focal seizures, as affective symptoms may occur in generalized epilepsies. The affective symptoms: fear, anxiety, panic, sadness, mystic, ecstatic, sexual, and mirth were identified, and semiology was evaluated in accordance with ILAE guidelines. Symptom onset, frequency, brain localization, and video-EEG monitoring technique were evaluated to assess localization and lateralization of regional networks. Scalp EEG, invasive EEG recordings, and electrical stimulation were analyzed. A total of 1276 documents were identified, with 98 studies included for review. Fear was the most frequently reported ictal emotion, identified in 274 patients (66%), predominantly in temporal lobe epilepsy. Sadness and sexual, ecstatic symptoms were also consistently associated with temporal networks. Mirth was more frequently reported with frontal onset seizures. Evidence, primarily from Class IV studies, reported right hemispheric predominance for ecstasy, mystic, and sexual symptoms at seizure onset. Ictal affective symptoms at seizure onset yield localizing information. The limbic system, especially the temporal lobe, followed by the frontal lobe, was the most common site of origin.
