Expanded repeat RNAs drive aberrant cortical network activity in benign adult familial myoclonus epilepsy.推荐

PMID 43025427 · Cell reports. Medicine · 2026 11 13
作者：Suga M, Kondo T, Imamura K, Nagafuku N et al.
链接：PubMed · DOI
命中检索：高影响力期刊 × Epilepsy
发表形式：期刊论文（研究设计待核对）
摘要：Expansions of short nucleotide repeats cause diverse neurological disorders, yet how they perturb neuronal function remains unclear. Benign adult familial myoclonus epilepsy (BAFME) is caused by intronic pentanucleotide repeat expansions in genes including SAMD12. Here, we establish patient-derived cortical organoids and a microelectrode array platform, coupled with a frequency-decomposition-based analytical framework, to characterize BAFME-associated neuronal hyperexcitability and compare organoid electrophysiological features with those observed in patient electroencephalographic recordings. Single-cell RNA sequencing reveals altered expression of genes related to calcium regulation and α-amino-3-hydroxy-5-methyl-4-isoxazolepropionic acid (AMPA) receptor function in BAFME cortical neurons. Consistent with these changes, BAFME organoids show an increased density of AMPA-receptor-positive synaptic puncta. Pharmacological AMPA receptor antagonism suppresses hyperexcitability. Antisense oligonucleotides targeting the expanded repeat RNA-which reduce pathogenic RNA foci without significantly affecting SAMD12 mRNA levels-also attenuate hyperexcitability. These findings indicate that targeting expanded repeat RNA mitigates aberrant cortical network activity in BAFME organoids.

摘要：OBJECTIVE: To characterize antiseizure medication (ASM) selection, subsequent ASM prescribing, and treatment pathways among patients with epilepsy receiving care within a pediatric health care system. METHODS: We conducted a retrospective electronic health record study over 17 years. Each unique ASM was counted once per patient, and ASMs were ordered by the date of their first observed prescription. For each observed ASM, we determined whether a subsequent unique ASM was prescribed during available follow-up. Prescription of a third unique ASM was used as a proxy for potential drug-resistant epilepsy. RESULTS: Among 20 009 patients, 27 ASMs and 3507 treatment pathways were identified. The most common first-observed ASMs were levetiracetam (35.1%), oxcarbazepine (17.2%), and valproic acid (12.3%). From 2010 to 2026, the proportion of first-observed prescriptions for levetiracetam increased from 23.1% to 41.5%. The proportion of all distinct ASMs first recorded for individual patients that were newer-generation ASMs increased from 0% in 2010 to 9.1% in 2026. The proportion of patients without a subsequent ASM ranged from 16.2% to 69.6% across commonly prescribed first-observed ASMs. Overall, 55.6% of patients had one unique ASM recorded, 22.4% had two, and 22.0% had at least three, thereby meeting the study's prescription-based proxy for potential drug-resistant epilepsy. Beyond the most common single- and two-ASM pathways, each remaining pathway accounted for less than 1% of the cohort. SIGNIFICANCE: Prescribing remained concentrated among established ASMs, although their relative use changed substantially. The proportion of patients without a subsequent ASM varied widely across medications, and treatment pathways beyond first-observed therapy were highly heterogeneous. These findings characterize real-world treatment patterns and identify a need for comparative effectiveness studies of ASM sequencing. PLAIN LANGUAGE SUMMARY: We examined antiseizure medication prescribing over 17 years among more than 20 000 patients with epilepsy receiving care in a pediatric health care system. More than half had only one medication recorded, whereas 22% had at least three medications; however, the available records could not determine whether these medications failed to control seizures. Levetiracetam became more commonly prescribed, newer medications remained a small proportion of overall use, and medication sequences varied widely after the first observed treatment. These findings highlight the need for better evidence to guide medication selection after initial treatment.

摘要：OBJECTIVE: Acute symptomatic seizures (ASyS) account for nearly half of new onset seizures, but evidence guiding management remains limited. We aimed to characterize international variation in ASyS management across common etiologies. METHODS: We conducted a cross-sectional, vignette-based survey through the International League Against Epilepsy national chapters (February to May 2025). Five etiology-based vignettes assessed clinical and electrographic ASyS scenario after ischemic stroke, traumatic brain injury (TBI), meningitis, hyponatremia, and alcohol withdrawal. Respondents who managed neonatal seizures completed a neonatal stroke vignette. We assessed antiseizure medication (ASM) initiation, duration after clinical ASyS, and continuous electroencephalography (cEEG) use. Interrespondent variability was quantified by normalized Shannon entropy (H'). Multivariable generalized estimating equations accounted for repeated responses. RESULTS: Among 963 respondents (92 countries), 62% recommended ASM initiation in clinical and 61% in electrographic ASyS scenarios. Overall ASM initiation variability was high (H' = .96), highest for alcohol withdrawal (H' = 1.0), followed by stroke (H' > .9), and consistently higher for electrographic than clinical ASyS. Etiology was the strongest driver of ASM initiation. Compared with stroke, ASM initiation odds were higher for TBI (odds ratio [OR] = 2.5, 95% confidence interval [CI] = 2.2-2.8) and meningitis (OR = 1.9, 95% CI = 1.7-2.2), and lower for hyponatremia (OR = .2, 95% CI = .2-.2) and alcohol withdrawal (OR = .5, 95% CI = .5-.6). Compared with Europe, initiation odds were higher in North America (OR = 2.7, 95% CI = 2.0-3.6), Asia-Oceania (OR = 2.4, 95% CI = 2.0-3.0), and Latin America (OR = 2.0, 95% CI = 1.5-2.7), but not Africa. These differences persisted after accounting for country income. Compared with stroke, the mean ASM duration was 3.2 months shorter for hyponatremia and .6 months longer for TBI. Alcohol withdrawal durations were polarized (41% discontinued at discharge; 20% continued >12 months). cEEG use was markedly higher in North America than Europe (OR = 11.3, 95% CI = 7.8-16.5). Among 291 neonatal respondents, 75% continued ASM beyond discharge. SIGNIFICANCE: ASyS management varies markedly, reaching near-maximal heterogeneity in several common scenarios. Although etiology drives decisions, geography and provider characteristics also influence care, underscoring an urgent need for randomized trials and international consensus guidance to harmonize care.

摘要：OBJECTIVE: The ketogenic diet treatment (KDT) is an established non-pharmacological treatment for drug-resistant epilepsy (DRE). 作者：Karatza P, Cserpan D, Gennari AG, Grubenmann A et al.
链接：PubMed · DOI
命中检索：癫痫专科刊 × Epilepsy

摘要：OBJECTIVE: Hypoxic-ischemic encephalopathy (HIE) is a leading cause of neonatal seizures, but seizure detection is challenging when continuous electroencephalography (EEG) is unavailable and neonates undergo only a single routine EEG. Biomarkers derived from routine EEG may aid identification of neonates with seizures. We investigated whether scalp high-frequency oscillation (HFO) rates derived from the first eligible routine EEG (1) differentiate neonates with HIE with and without seizures; and (2) contribute to seizure classification alongside established clinical, neurophysiological, and imaging biomarkers. METHODS: We retrospectively analyzed routine scalp EEG recordings from 33 term neonates with HIE (17 with seizures, 16 without), obtained at a mean postnatal age of 4 ± 3 days. The mean analyzed EEG duration was 38 ± 14 min in neonates with seizures and 36 ± 20 min in those without. HFO rates in sleep segments were quantified using a validated automated detector. RESULTS: Scalp HFO rates were significantly higher in neonates with seizures than in those without (.12 ± .11 vs. .03 ± .02 HFOs/min/channel, adjusted p = .02). An HFO rate threshold of .07 HFOs/min/channel optimally discriminated neonates who had seizures from those who did not (area under the curve [AUC] = .81). HFO rates did not differ significantly according to HIE severity, therapeutic hypothermia, antiseizure medication response, EEG background activity, or magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) abnormalities. Multivariable models incorporating HFO rate, EEG background activity, and MRI findings showed discrimination (AUC = .92, 95% confidence interval [CI] = .69-1.00 for logistic regression; AUC = 1.00, 95% CI = .75-1.00 for random forest), with HFO rate as the strongest contributor to seizure classification. 摘要：Neurodegenerative disorders, including Alzheimer's disease, Parkinson's disease, Huntington's disease, amyotrophic lateral sclerosis, multiple sclerosis and stroke, are characterized by progressive neuronal dysfunction, synaptic impairment and neural circuit degeneration, leading to severe cognitive and motor deficits. Although considerable progress has been made in understanding their molecular pathology, the circuit level mechanisms driving disease onset and progression remain incompletely understood. Optogenetics, which combines microbial opsins, genetic engineering and optical stimulation, enables reversible, cell-specific control of neuronal activity with millisecond precision and has transformed neuroscience from observational analysis to causal investigation of neural circuits. This review summarizes recent advances in the molecular basis of optogenetics, microbial opsins, gene delivery strategies and optical stimulation technologies, together with their applications in basic neurobiology and neurodegenerative disease research. We discuss how optogenetics has advanced understanding of neural circuit organization, synaptic plasticity, memory, neurodevelopment, sleep regulation, protein aggregation, synaptic dysfunction, neuroinflammation, mitochondrial impairment, oxidative stress and regulated cell death. Emerging therapeutic strategies, including closed-loop neuromodulation, wireless photostimulation, artificial intelligence-assisted neural decoding, nanotechnology enabled light delivery and brain computer interfaces, are critically evaluated with emphasis on their current evidence status, translational challenges and limitations for clinical implementation. Collectively, optogenetics provides an unprecedented platform for deciphering disease mechanisms and evaluating circuit-based neuromodulation strategies, while continued technological and biological validation will determine its future role in precision therapies for neurodegenerative disorders.
