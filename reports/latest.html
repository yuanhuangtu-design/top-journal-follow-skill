摘要：Cyclin-dependent kinase-like 5 (CDKL5) is a serine/threonine kinase essential for brain development and function. Mutations in CDKL5 cause CDKL5 deficiency disorder (CDD), a severe early-onset epileptic encephalopathy associated with impaired synapse formation and function. However, how CDKL5 mutations disrupt synaptic function remains unclear. Here, we show that the major neuronal isoform of CDKL5 contains a C-terminal PDZ domain-binding motif that mediates direct binding to PSD-95 and SHANK PDZ domains and recruits CDKL5 to the postsynaptic density. Disrupting this motif mislocalized CDKL5, reduced dendritic spine density, and impaired spine maturation. We also show that the CDKL5 kinase domain binds the R8 rod domain of the mechanosensitive scaffold talin. Mutation of the talin-binding interface or reduced cellular contractility altered CDKL5 positioning relative to focal adhesions. These findings define how CDKL5 is targeted to synapses, suggest that talin mechanoswitching may position CDKL5 kinase activity, and highlight the importance of C-terminal anchoring for interpreting pathogenic truncations.

摘要：Mutations inSPTAN1, the gene encoding the neuronal cytoskeletal protein αII-spectrin, are associated with epilepsy and movement disorders, complicating the clinical interpretation of seizure-like behaviors. Stress-induced paroxysmal motor events can mimic behaviors typically observed in epileptic seizures, yet they do not exhibit definitive electrophysiological correlates. This study investigated the link between seizure-like behaviors and the brain's cortical electroencephalographic (EEG) activity in heterozygousSptan1R1098Q mice, a novel model of spectrin-related neurodevelopmental disease. Mutant and wild-type (WT) mice were subject to automated gait analysis, long-term video-EEG monitoring, and a handling-induced seizure susceptibility (HISS) paradigm at various ages. R1098Q mice demonstrated pronounced gait abnormalities characteristic of ataxia as well as age-related increases in delta and theta power, and spike-wave discharges (SWD), suggesting elevated network excitability. During the HISS test, nearly all mutant mice exhibited significant seizure-like motor responses, such as forelimb clonus, impaired balance, and falls, whereas WT mice displayed no abnormal reactions. Notably, these motor behaviors did not coincide with cortical ictal EEG activity. Rather, stress induced a transient suppression of cortical EEG spiking, followed by the return of interictal spiking and SWDs. The present findings demonstrate a dissociation between seizure-like behaviors and cortical ictal EEG activity inSptan1R1098Q mice. These findings emphasize the limitations inherent to classifying seizures based solely on behavioral observations and underscore the necessity of incorporating electrophysiological data when evaluating seizure-like manifestations in genetic epilepsy models characterized by significant motor dysfunction. Such differentiation holds substantial relevance for preclinical epilepsy research and informs the interpretation of seizure-like events within neurogenetic disorders.

摘要：BACKGROUND: Pregnancy is a critical period for women with epilepsy (WWE), which requires careful planning, preconception care, and specialized care. METHODS: We conducted a cross-sectional study of WWE who had been pregnant at least once following epilepsy diagnosis. 摘要：OBJECTIVES: Anxiety and depression are common in epilepsy and often undertreated, yet little is known about epilepsy patient experiences of mental health care and patient-level barriers and facilitators to mental health care.
