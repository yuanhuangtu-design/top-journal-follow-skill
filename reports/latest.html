摘要：The neural extracellular matrix (ECM) is increasingly recognized as an active regulator of brain function and an important contributor to the pathogenesis of neuropsychiatric disorders. This Review examines ECM organization, function and adaptive remodelling in the healthy brain and synthesizes evidence for its pathophysiological alterations in schizophrenia, major depressive disorder and bipolar disorder. Based on findings from human studies and animal models, we propose an ECM-extended conceptual framework of major psychiatric diseases in which polygenic liability, stress exposure and neuroinflammatory signalling converge to remodel the ECM in circuits supporting reward processing, emotion regulation, cognitive control and social cognition. In this model, an early phase of protease-dominant ECM loosening destabilizes microcircuits composed of parvalbumin-expressing inhibitory neurons and principal cells whereas later reactive ECM rebuilding stabilizes dysfunctional network states and contributes to disease persistence, relapse and treatment resistance. This framework positions the ECM as a central hub of genetic, glial, synaptic and systems-level pathogenesis and suggests avenues for the development of ECM-sensitive biomarkers and ECM-directed interventions that may improve the treatment of these disorders.
中文摘要：神经细胞外基质（ECM）正日益被认为是脑功能的活性调节因子，也是神经精神疾病发病机制的重要影响因素。本综述探讨了健康大脑中ECM的组织、功能和适应性重塑，并综合了其在精神分裂症、重度抑郁障碍和双相情感障碍中的病理生理学改变的证据。基于人类研究的结果和 动物模型，我们提出了一个以ECM为基础的重大精神疾病概念框架，其中多基因易感性、应激暴露和神经炎症信号传导共同作用，重塑支持奖赏处理、情绪调节、认知控制和社会认知的神经环路中的ECM。在这个模型中，蛋白酶占主导地位的ECM松弛的早期阶段会破坏由表达小白蛋白的抑制性神经元组成的微回路的稳定性， 和主要细胞，而后期反应性ECM重建则稳定了功能失调的网络状态，并导致疾病的持续存在、复发和耐药性。该框架将ECM定位为遗传、神经胶质、突触和系统层面发病机制的中心枢纽，并提出了开发ECM敏感性生物标志物和ECM靶向干预措施的途径，这些措施可能改善这些疾病的治疗。
