Impact of entry trajectory angle-to-skull on electrode placement accuracy in robot-assisted stereoelectroencephalography.推荐
进入轨迹角度对颅骨对机器人辅助立体脑电图中电极放置精度的影响。
PMID 42913322 · Epilepsy research · 2026 Oct 14
作者：Park C, Kim JH, Kim JH, Shin JH et al.
链接：PubMed · DOI
命中检索：癫痫专科刊 × Epilepsy
摘要：OBJECTIVE: Despite the widespread use of robotic systems to improve stereotactic accuracy in stereoelectroencephalography (SEEG), targeting errors still occur. Several studies have suggested that the trajectory-to-skull angle is closely associated with targeting error among various influencing factors; however, quantitative analyses remain limited. Therefore, this study aimed to quantitatively evaluate the relationship between trajectory-to-skull angle and implantation accuracy and to establish practical cut-off values for surgical planning. METHODS: A total of 10 patients (103 electrodes) were included in this study and various clinical variables were analyzed to identify whether trajectory-to-skull angle has a significant relation with accuracy using multivariate linear regression. Then, receiver operating characteristic (ROC) curve analysis with Youden index optimization was applied to determine angle thresholds for achieving ≤ 3 mm accuracy. RESULTS: Multivariate analysis showed that both axial and coronal trajectory-to-skull angles were significantly associated with target error, with more tangential incidence angles corresponding to larger deviations (p < 0.01). Axial angle-to-skull was particularly associated with anteroposterior (Y-axis) error, whereas coronal angle-to-skull primarily predicted superoinferior (Z-axis) error. ROC curve analysis identified optimal angle thresholds of 11.8° for axial trajectories and 6.3° for coronal trajectories to predict targeting accuracy within 3 mm. When both axial and coronal angles were maintained within these cut-offs, the probability of achieving a target error ≤ 3 mm was 88.2%. CONCLUSION: Trajectory-to-skull angle is a key geometric determinant of electrode placement accuracy in robot-assisted SEEG. Quantitative angle thresholds identified in this study provide practical guidance for trajectory planning and may help minimize mechanical deviation, improve implantation safety, and enhance the reliability of seizure-network localization.

摘要：INTRODUCTION: Recent evidence suggests that dehydrodolichol diphosphate synthase (DHDDS, MIM*608,172) gene variation can cause neurological diseases. However, the complete clinical and genetic spectrum of DHDDS-related disorders has not been fully elucidated. This study aims to systematically describe the clinical and genetic features of five patients with molecularly confirmed DHDDS variants and to provide an updated review of previously reported cases. METHODS: We retrospectively enrolled patients with DHDDS gene variants in the Epilepsy Center of Beijing Tiantan Hospital, Capital Medical University from 2021 to 2023. Clinical, neuroimaging, electrophysiological and genetic data were collected. Whole‑exome sequencing and Sanger validation were performed for variant identification and ACMG‑based classification. PubMed was searched using the term "DHDDS" to identify previously reported cases. The clinical and genetic characteristics of the eligible cases were summarized descriptively. RESULTS: Five patients in our hospital with two novel DHDDS variants (c.623G>A, p.G208E and c.697C>A, p.P233T) were reported. A total of 74 patients (3 from our center and 71 from the literature) carrying pathogenic or likely pathogenic variants were collected for the final analysis. The most prevalent variants were c.632G>A (p.R211Q, n = 26, 35.14%), c.110G>A (p.R37H, n = 16, 21.62%), c.109C>T (p.R37C, n = 9, 12.16%), and c.614G>A (p.R205Q, n = 9). The most common clinical manifestations included epilepsy (n = 64, 86.49%), intellectual disability (n = 62, 83.78%), ataxia (n = 50, 67.57%), global developmental delay (n = 50, 67.57%), tremor (n = 48, 64.86%), and myoclonus (n = 44, 59.46%). No significant phenotypic differences were observed among patients carrying different variants. CONCLUSION: This study expanded the phenotypic spectrum of diseases related to DHDDS and reported two new variations. For cases of developmental epileptic encephalopathy of unknown cause, progressive myoclonus or complex movement disorders, DHDDS gene screening should be considered.

摘要：BACKGROUND: Epilepsy affects over 50 million people worldwide, and approximately one third of patients develop drug-resistant epilepsy (DRE), a major driver of morbidity, mortality, and healthcare costs. Vagus nerve stimulation (VNS) is an established neuromodulatory treatment option for DRE, but local guidance on its use in the United Arab Emirates (UAE) remains limited. This study aimed to establish an expert consensus to define best practices for the effective and efficient use of the VNS in the UAE setting. METHODS: A multidisciplinary Task Force conducted a literature review and developed 25 evidence- and experience-informed statements, which were refined during an in-person expert meeting to ensure clinical relevance for the UAE context. Statements were rated through a single-round modified Delphi process involving UAE neurologists and neurosurgeons. Participants rated their agreement with each statement using a 7-point Likert scale. Consensus was predefined as ≥ 70% agreement, corresponding to ratings of 6-7. Statements with 60-69% agreement were classified as moderate agreement but were not considered to have reached consensus. RESULTS: Fifty-six experts completed the Delphi round, corresponding to a 75.7% response rate. Item-level denominators varied across statements. Eighteen of 25 statements reached consensus, six demonstrated moderate agreement, and one did not reach consensus. Strong agreement was observed for patient selection, safety, structured parameter optimization, and multidisciplinary care pathways. Moderate agreement emerged for presurgical evaluation, timing of implantation, and selected clinical scenarios. CONCLUSION: Building on existing international guidance and previous Delphi work in other settings, this study provides a UAE-specific description of expert agreement and variability on the clinical and organizational use of VNS for DRE. The findings may inform future local pathway development and identify areas requiring clearer definitions, prospective data collection, and further evidence generation. PLAIN LANGUAGE SUMMARY: This study asked expert clinicians in the United Arab Emirates to rate 25 statements on the clinical and organizational use of vagus nerve stimulation for drug-resistant epilepsy. Vagus nerve stimulation is an adjunctive treatment that aims to reduce seizure burden and improve patient-centred outcomes; it should not be interpreted primarily as a treatment intended to achieve complete seizure freedom. Eighteen statements reached the prespecified consensus threshold, while six showed moderate agreement and one did not reach consensus. Experts showed agreement on several areas, including multidisciplinary care, safety considerations, MRI compatibility, structured follow-up, and individualized programming. However, agreement was more limited for some topics, including expected responder rates after one year, timing of implantation, selected programming criteria, and quality-of-life effects independent of seizure reduction. These findings identify areas where expert views are aligned and areas where practice remains variable. They may support the development of more consistent local care pathways, while highlighting topics that require further clinical evidence and local guidance.

摘要：OBJECTIVE: Cognitive impairment is cited as one of the most consequential comorbidities of epilepsy. Cognitive difficulties may be managed using compensatory strategies. However, little is understood about how these strategies are used by adults with epilepsy. The Everyday Compensation (EComp) Questionnaire offers a means of assessing the use of these strategies but has only been validated for neurologically healthy older adults. The present study examined the preliminary psychometric properties of the EComp Questionnaire in epilepsy. METHODS: 37 adults with focal epilepsy participating in a randomized controlled trial of a cognitive prehabilitation program completed the EComp Questionnaire, Quality of Life Epilepsy Inventory-31 (QOLIE-31), and the Instrumental Activities of Daily-Living Scale (IADL-C) at baseline and follow-up. Analyses included evaluation of internal consistency, construct validity, test-retest reliability, and item-level performance. RESULTS: Across 20 imputed datasets, the EComp Questionnaire demonstrated acceptable Cronbach's α values. Test-retest ICCs were moderate (0.50-.58) but imprecise, with 95 % CIs spanning poor to good reliability. Correlations between the EComp Questionnaire and the IADL-C (ρ = 0.34, p = 0.04) and QOLIE-31 (r = -0.43, p = 0.007) suggested support for construct validity. Select items demonstrated low endorsement and weak association with the total score. SIGNIFICANCE: Findings provide preliminary support for the EComp as a tool for assessing compensatory strategy use in adults with epilepsy. Understanding these adaptive behaviors and functional needs can assist providers in formulating treatment plans and interventions. Future research with larger and diverse epilepsy samples will be necessary to confirm structural validity.

摘要：PURPOSE: Childhood epilepsy imposes psychological burden not only on the affected child but also on caregivers. This study aimed to compare parenting stress and life satisfaction between parents of children and adolescents with epilepsy and parents of children and adolescents without a chronic condition, and to identify demographic and clinical factors associated with these outcomes among parents of children and adolescents with epilepsy. METHODS: This cross-sectional study included 58 parents of children and adolescents aged 9-19 years with epilepsy and 44 parents of hospital-based control children and adolescents without a chronic condition (N = 102). Questionnaires were completed by the responding parent, typically whichever parent accompanied the child or adolescent to the clinical visit. Parenting stress and life satisfaction were assessed using the Parenting Stress Index-Short Form (PSI-SF) and the Satisfaction With Life Scale (SWLS), respectively. The SWLS captured the responding parent's own global life satisfaction. Group comparisons were performed using Welch's t test or the Mann-Whitney U test, as appropriate. Multiple linear regression was used to identify factors independently associated with parenting stress and life satisfaction, with Holm-Bonferroni correction applied for multiple comparisons. RESULTS: Parents in the epilepsy group reported higher PSI-SF total scores than controls (84.88 ± 24.04 vs. 72.73 ± 18.61, P = 0.005, Hedges' g = 0.55); among the subscales, the difference in Parental Distress remained significant after Holm-Bonferroni correction, whereas the differences in Parent-Child Dysfunctional Interaction and Difficult Child did not. Life satisfaction did not differ significantly between groups (22.77 ± 7.00 vs. 23.98 ± 7.49, P = 0.442), and the confidence interval did not exclude a meaningful difference. Group membership remained associated with parenting stress after adjustment for the measured demographic covariates (child/adolescent age and sex, paternal education, and economic status) (b = 12.13, P = 0.010). Within the epilepsy group, parents of children with a seizure reported within the preceding 6 months had higher PSI-SF scores than parents of children without such a seizure (102.1 ± 22.0 vs. 81.1 ± 23.0, P = 0.027); all clinical analyses were exploratory. CONCLUSION: In this hospital-based sample, parents of children and adolescents with epilepsy reported higher PSI-SF scores than parents of controls, whereas findings for life satisfaction and for seizure-related determinants were inconclusive or exploratory.
