摘要：The amygdala and hippocampus are key structures in emotional processing, yet the transient neural dynamics coordinating their interaction remain poorly understood. While conventional spectral analysis revealed no valence-specific patterns, beta bursts (13-30 Hz)-transient, high-power events-were selectively modified by task features. In both regions, beta bursts were associated with increased gamma amplitude and enhanced phase coherence, with beta-gamma phase-amplitude coupling capturing emotion-related dynamics. Hippocampal bursts showed no reciprocal influence. These findings suggest that beta bursts may represent a temporally precise neural correlate of emotional appraisal and a potential target for neuromodulation in mood disorders.

摘要：OBJECTIVE: Peri-ictal magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) abnormalities (PMA) are increasingly recognized in patients with status epilepticus (SE), but the clinical factors influencing their occurrence and extent remain incompletely understood. We aimed to quantify PMA and investigate the associations of PMA occurrence and volume with SE duration, etiology, and mortality. METHODS: In this multicenter cohort study, comprising a retrospectively identified cohort and a prospectively recruited cohort, adult patients with SE who underwent brain MRI within 48 h of SE onset were included. PMA were identified on diffusion-weighted imaging (DWI) and quantified using an automated postprocessing pipeline to calculate total PMA volume. Multivariable regression analyses were used to examine factors associated with PMA occurrence, extent, and mortality. RESULTS: Of 260 patients, 91 (35%) had PMA on DWI. In multivariable analyses, SE duration and etiology were independently associated with PMA occurrence and volume. 摘要：OBJECTIVE: To explore whether preoperative individualized morphometric networks derived from structural MRI may provide discriminative information for predicting surgical outcomes in magnetic resonance imaging (MRI)-negative epilepsy. METHODS: Thirty-one MRI-negative epilepsy patients who underwent epilepsy surgery were retrospectively included (17 seizure-free and 14 with seizure-recurrence) in this exploratory study. Individualized gray matter morphometric networks were constructed from preoperative T1-weighted MRI based on cortical thickness distribution features. Network-based statistics were used to identify outcome-related subnetworks. Graph-theoretical features extracted from these subnetworks were classified using a random vector functional link (RVFL) network. Model performance was evaluated using the area under the receiver operating characteristic curve (AUC). RESULTS: Graph-theoretical analysis revealed differences in network properties between seizure-free and seizure-recurrence groups, particularly in degree centrality and nodal clustering coefficient of the right orbital middle frontal gyrus, left inferior occipital gyrus, and left fusiform gyrus (p < .05). The combined graph-theoretical model demonstrated the highest discriminative performance among the evaluated imaging models (AUC = .876) and outperformed the clinical-only benchmark model (AUC = .504), although performance estimates showed variability across repeated splits.
