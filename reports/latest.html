摘要：The amygdala and hippocampus are key structures in emotional processing, yet the transient neural dynamics coordinating their interaction remain poorly understood. We simultaneously recorded single-neuron activity and local field potentials from these regions in epilepsy patients performing an emotional image-rating task. Neurons in both regions exhibited firing rate changes predictive of subjective emotional valence. While conventional spectral analysis revealed no valence-specific patterns, beta bursts (13-30 Hz)-transient, high-power events-were selectively modified by task features. In both regions, beta bursts were associated with increased gamma amplitude and enhanced phase coherence, with beta-gamma phase-amplitude coupling capturing emotion-related dynamics. Critically, amygdala beta bursts were associated with valence-dependent, bidirectional modulation of hippocampal interneuron firing - decreased during very unpleasant/highest arousal processing and increased during high-arousal very pleasant processing - while putative pyramidal-cell firing did not change significantly. Hippocampal bursts showed no reciprocal influence. These findings suggest that beta bursts may represent a temporally precise neural correlate of emotional appraisal and a potential target for neuromodulation in mood disorders.

摘要：People with epilepsy experience daily oscillations in seizure risk. Using a video-tracking model to automate detection, we explored mechanisms underlying spontaneous seizures in Drosophila and find that flies, like people, exhibit daily oscillations in seizure risk. These rhythms are absent in flies lacking a molecular circadian clock and are independent of sleep. Thermogenetic screening and cell-specific clock perturbations reveal that the ventrolateral neurons, including "morning cells", and dorsolateral neurons, comprising the "evening cells", modulate diurnal oscillations in seizure risk. Pigment-dispersing factor and short neuropeptide F from morning cells increase seizure risk during the day. During this time, morning cell activity increases whole-brain activity. Our findings reveal an unexpected role for brain clock neurons in modulating daily seizure risk.

摘要：BACKGROUND AND OBJECTIVES: Cenobamate (CNB) is an antiseizure medication (ASM) with demonstrated efficacy in drug-resistant focal epilepsy, but its role in status epilepticus (SE) remains unclear. Emerging evidence suggests that it may achieve early clinically meaningful seizure control at relatively low doses, with an acceptable safety profile, supporting its potential utility as an urgent adjunctive therapy in severe refractory epilepsy. We aimed to evaluate the effectiveness and safety of CNB for the treatment of focal-onset refractory SE (RSE) and super-refractory SE (SRSE) in real-world clinical practice. METHODS: We conducted a multicenter retrospective cohort study using a prospectively maintained registry including adult patients with focal RSE and SRSE treated between January 2022 and October 2025. Clinical, EEG, and treatment data were collected. The primary outcome was sustained SE termination (>24 hours) with electroclinical resolution attributable to CNB. A secondary propensity score-matched analysis (1:2) compared CNB-exposed patients with matched nonexposed controls from the same registry (2011-2025), using target trial emulation principles. RESULTS: Thirty-one patients received CNB (mean age 61 ± 21 years; 54.8% male); 45.2% met criteria for SRSE. CNB was initiated a median of 4 days (interquartile range [IQR] 2-12) after SE onset after failure of 5 ASMs (IQR 4-5), at a median starting dose of 50 mg/d (IQR 25-50). 摘要：OBJECTIVE: Neurobehavioral therapy (NBT) is a multi-modal treatment that combines elements of interpersonal, dialectical, and cognitive behavioral therapy. METHODS: We conducted a retrospective chart review of 43 patients who received NBT for FDS. This study supports the value of psychotherapy as a primary or adjunct treatment modality for patients with FDS.

摘要：PURPOSE: Magnetoencephalography (MEG) is increasingly used in the presurgical evaluation of drug-resistant epilepsy (DRE), particularly in MRI-negative cases. However, the relationship between MEG localization, intracranial EEG (iEEG) concordance, underlying pathology, and surgical outcomes remains incompletely defined. METHODS: We performed a retrospective cohort study of 51 patients with MRI-negative DRE who underwent MEG and iEEG evaluation at our center from 2009 to 2025. MEG spike source localization was categorized as complete, partial, or discordant relative to the iEEG-defined SOZ using a lobar-level concordance framework. Surgical outcomes were assessed in 29 patients who underwent resective surgery using the Engel classification at 6 months, 1 year, 2 years, and last follow-up. Associations between concordance, pathology, and outcomes were evaluated using chi-square or Fisher's exact tests as appropriate, with Monte-Carlo exact inference used for sparse contingency tables. RESULTS: MEG identified localizing interictal abnormalities in 33 of 51 patients (65%), while 18 (35%) had no MEG spikes. Among 32 patients with both localizing MEG and an iEEG-defined SOZ, complete lobar-level concordance was observed in 23 (72%), partial concordance in 7 (22%), and discordance in 2 (6%). At 1 year after resection, Engel class I outcomes were observed in 10 of 11 patients (91%) with complete concordance, 5 of 5 (100%) with partial concordance, and 5 of 8 patients (63%) with MEG-negative studies. Exploratory Monte-Carlo exact analysis demonstrated a significant impact of histopathology on the distribution of MEG-iEEG concordance (p = 0.004). Histopathology was not a significant predictor of study MEG positivity. CONCLUSIONS: MEG identified localizing interictal abnormalities in approximately two-thirds of this selected cohort of patients with MRI-negative DRE. When MEG was localizing and an iEEG-defined SOZ was identified, lobar-level concordance was common.
