摘要：OBJECTIVES: Management of anti-seizure medications (ASMs) after hospitalization for status epilepticus (SE) is poorly defined, particularly regarding outpatient tapering. We aimed to identify factors associated with ASM tapering and compare seizure recurrence between early and late taper strategies. METHODS: We conducted a single-center retrospective study of adults hospitalized for SE between 2011 and 2021 with at least 1 year of outpatient follow-up. Statistical analyses included Wilcoxon rank-sum tests, Pearson's chi-square or Fisher's exact tests, and multivariable logistic regression to evaluate factors associated with ASM tapering within 1-2 years and seizure recurrence. RESULTS: Among 282 patients (mean age 55 ± 18 years; 47% female), 37.9% underwent outpatient ASM tapering, of whom 83.7% remained seizure-free for at least 6 months. Early tapering (≤1 year) was associated with higher seizure recurrence compared with late tapering (>1 year) (28.2% vs. 2.7%, p < 0.001). None of eight patients tapered after 2 years experienced recurrence within 6 months. Nonconvulsive SE (odds ratio [OR] 2.21 [1.28-3.84], p = 0.005) and acute symptomatic SE (OR 1.79 [1.01-3.16], p = 0.045) were independently associated with tapering. Most patients who tapered (73.8%) had short SE duration (≤1 day). Acute symptomatic SE was associated with lower 6-months seizure recurrence Odds, approaching significance (adjusted OR 0.27 [0.07-1.03], p = 0.055). Longer SE duration (6-10 days vs. <1 day) was associated with higher odds of recurrence in univariable but not multivariable analysis. SE etiology, number of prehospital ASMs, comorbidities, and outpatient EEG completion were not associated with tapering or recurrence. SIGNIFICANCE: Despite a relatively low seizure recurrence rate, fewer than 40% of patients underwent ASM tapering. These findings suggest supervised tapering may be reasonable beginning 1 year after SE, particularly in patients with shorter SE duration. Prospective studies are needed to refine tapering strategies. Fewer than 40% had their medications tapered, and most remained seizure-free for at least 6 months. 摘要：The peripheral nervous system has emerged as an important regulator of cancer biology. Once considered passive bystanders, tumour-infiltrating neurites are now recognized as active components of the tumour microenvironment that influence tumour progression through direct and indirect interactions with malignant, immune, stromal, glial and vascular cells. As the field of cancer neuroscience continues to advance, the nervous system is increasingly recognized not only as a regulator of tumour behaviour but also as a promising target for therapeutic intervention.
