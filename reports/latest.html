HD-tACS at 40 Hz Improves Consciousness and Brain Connectivity in Prolonged Disorders of Consciousness: A Randomized Sham-Controlled Clinical Trial.推荐
40 Hz HD-tACS改善长期意识障碍中的意识和脑连接性：一项随机假对照临床试验。
PMID 42829016 · NeuroImage · 2026 Oct 3
作者：Hu J, Mou C, Jiang X, Zhang L et al.
链接：PubMed · DOI
命中检索：脑电信号分析方法
摘要：BACKGROUND: Frequency-specific high-definition transcranial alternating current stimulation (HD-tACS) may facilitate recovery in patients with prolonged disorders of consciousness (pDoC), but predictive biomarkers and mechanistic insights are limited. OBJECTIVE: To investigate the therapeutic efficacy of 40 Hz or 4 Hz HD-tACS in pDoC. METHODS: Sixty pDoC patients were randomized into four groups receiving 40 Hz or 4 Hz HD-tACS, each with a corresponding sham condition. Behavioral recovery was assessed via Coma Recovery Scale-Revised (CRS-R), network connectivity was analyzed with electroencephalography (EEG), and baseline 40 Hz auditory steady-state response (ASSR) was explored as a predictive biomarker. RESULTS: Five days of 40 Hz HD-tACS yielded greater improvements of total CRS-R scores than 4 Hz or sham, particularly in visual and arousal subscales. EEG revealed strengthened short- and long-range connectivity quantified by phase-locking value (PLV), correlating with behavioral gains. At six months, 37.5% of 40 Hz patients got a good outcome with Glasgow Outcome Scale-Extended (GOS-E) score ≥ 4, which was significantly higher than that observed in the sham group (P = 0.019). Higher baseline ASSR predicted stronger network enhancement and clinical improvement. CONCLUSIONS: 40 Hz HD-tACS promotes recovery and network reorganization in pDoC. Baseline ASSR may provide complementary information for understanding individual variability in responses to frequency-specific neuromodulation.
