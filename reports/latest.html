摘要：BACKGROUND AND OBJECTIVES: Individuals with epilepsy face an elevated risk of suicide, but risk factors for suicide remain incompletely understood. We conducted a nationwide case-control study in Sweden to investigate clinical characteristics associated with suicide risk in persons with epilepsy. METHODS: Persons with epilepsy were identified through Swedish nationwide registers, with epilepsy diagnoses validated through medical record review. Cases were individuals who died by suicide between 2006 and 2011, validated through autopsy reports, and controls were derived from the source population using similar validation procedures. Characteristics included epilepsy type, etiology, tonic-clonic seizure (TCS) frequency, antiseizure medication (ASM) treatment, living conditions, and psychiatric comorbidity. Odds ratios (ORs) with 95% CIs were estimated using conditional logistic regression. RESULTS: The study included 111 suicide cases and 1,147 controls. Suicide cases had a mean age at epilepsy onset of 20 years, and 35% were female. Alcohol abuse (OR 11.81, 95% CI 6.25-22.30) and other substance abuse (OR 10.71, 95% CI 4.52-25.38) showed stronger associations than depression (OR 2.94, 95% CI 1.56-5.53) and anxiety disorders (OR 2.93, 95% CI 1.57-5.44). Living alone was associated with increased risk (OR 2.59, 95% CI 1.58-4.26). Recent TCS showed a dose-response relationship, with the highest risk among individuals experiencing 4-10 TCS in the preceding year (OR 5.01, 95% CI 2.2-11.3). Structural and unknown epilepsy etiologies were associated with elevated risk (OR 3.07, 95% CI 1.0-9.5 and OR 8.80, 95% CI 2.9-27.1, respectively). ASM treatment was associated with lower suicide risk (monotherapy OR 0.51, 95% CI 0.27-0.95; polytherapy OR 0.21, 95% CI 0.1-0.5), and nonadherence was associated with higher risk (OR 3.02, 95% CI 1.8-5.1). DISCUSSION: This study identified a distinct risk profile for suicide in persons with epilepsy, highlighting alcohol and substance abuse, living alone, seizure burden, nonadherence, and structural or unknown etiologies as key risk factors. The stronger associations for substance abuse may reflect characteristics specific to epilepsy populations and the focus on completed suicide. Screening for substance abuse and improved seizure control may contribute to suicide prevention. Major limitations include the modest number of cases and incomplete information on prior suicidal behavior.

摘要：Voltage-gated sodium channels regulate neuronal excitability and synaptic transmission in the postnatal and adult brain. The gene SCN2A, encoding the sodium channel Nav1.2, regulates synaptic development and variants in SCN2A are associated with autism spectrum disorders (ASD) and a broad spectrum of epilepsy phenotypes, including early-onset developmental and epileptic encephalopathies. The expression pattern of SCN2A begins during prenatal cortical development, prior to the onset of synaptic transmission, but it is unknown whether SCN2A regulates early cortical development through mechanisms independent of synaptic transmission. Here we reveal that isogenic and ASD patient-derived human forebrain organoids modelling a loss of SCN2A function display impaired excitatory and inhibitory neurogenesis, leading to a developmental imbalance. Unexpectedly, we find precocious generation of cortical inhibitory neurons is driven by elevated Sonic hedgehog signaling and is reversible through pharmacological inhibition. Functionally, these developmental phenotypes arise due to Nav1.2-dependent sodium channel dysfunction and reduced action potential generation, leading to abnormal neuronal network activity. Our results identify a mechanism for cortical excitatory and inhibitory neurogenesis involving SCN2A, and reveal that early neurogenesis deficits precede postnatal neural circuit dysfunction in SCN2A-associated disorders.

OBJECTIVE: To determine efficacy and safety of the novel, potent KV7 channel opener azetukalner for treatment of focal seizures. DESIGN, SETTING, AND PARTICIPANTS: This phase 3, placebo-controlled randomized clinical trial of azetukalner was conducted over a 12-week double-blind period (November 18, 2022, to February 3, 2026) at 138 sites in North America, South America, Europe, and Asia Pacific. 摘要：PURPOSE: To compare antiseizure medication (ASM) treatment patterns among women with epilepsy (WWE) of reproductive age and pregnant WWE using nationwide administrative data and a university epilepsy referral center cohort in Poland. METHODS: This retrospective study analyzed two complementary datasets. Nationwide data were obtained from the Polish National Health Fund, including 44,951 WWE aged 20-49 years in 2024 and 594 pregnant WWE who delivered in 2022. Referral center data were collected from medical records of 277 WWE aged 20-49 years treated in 2024 and 112 pregnancies managed between 2019 and 2026. Sensitivity analyses confirmed the stability of the findings, whereas meta-regression showed no significant effect of age. The pooled adverse-event analysis of two implanted VNS trials showed no statistically significant between-group difference, although estimates were highly imprecise. Overall certainty of evidence was moderate. CONCLUSION: Closed-loop or movement-synchronized VNS may provide clinically meaningful improvements in upper-limb motor function after stroke; however, the available safety evidence is limited and imprecise, particularly for implanted VNS, and cannot be generalized to noninvasive transcutaneous auricular VNS.
