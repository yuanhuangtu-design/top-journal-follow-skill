摘要：OBJECTIVE: RAP-219, a potent selective negative allosteric modulator (NAM) of α-amino-3-hydroxy-5-methyl-4-isoxazolepropionic acid receptor (AMPAR) associated with γ8 transmembrane AMPAR regulatory protein (TARPγ8), is in phase 3 development for refractory focal epilepsy. This study was undertaken to assess efficacy and motor function following administration of AMPAR/TARPγ8 NAMs. METHODS: Single-dose RAP-219 (per os) efficacy was assessed in intravenous pentylenetetrazol and corneal kindling (CK) antiseizure mouse models. Single- and 7-day (once daily, per os) efficacy of a RAP-219 analog, RTX-1738, was assessed in the CK model. Pharmacokinetic and receptor occupancy (RO) studies were conducted to assess the plasma exposure and RO required for preclinical efficacy. Time to first twitch and onset of sustained clonus were recorded during intravenous pentylenetetrazol infusion following RAP-219 administration. Fully kindled mice received RAP-219 or RTX-1738 prior to corneal stimulation challenge. In parallel, plasma drug concentrations were measured in satellite groups. Motor impairment was assessed by rotarod. Hippocampal RO was determined via ex vivo autoradiography in mice. RESULTS: In the pentylenetetrazol model, .1- and 1-mg/kg RAP-219 doses were associated with higher seizure threshold versus vehicle (p < .01). Kindled mice were fully protected from seizures by RAP-219 (median effective dose [ED 50 ] = .02 mg/kg, median effective concentration [EC 50 ] = 2.3 ng/mL) with minimal, non-dose-dependent rotarod failures (therapeutic index > 150). RTX-1738 administration (single or multiple day) protected kindled mice from seizures with similar efficacy and potency (single dose: ED 50 = .05 mg/kg, EC 50 = 4.7 ng/mL; multiple-day administration: ED 50 = .02 mg/kg, EC 50 = 3.6 ng/mL). RAP-219 RO EC 50 in mice was 1.5 ng/mL, showing good correspondence of plasma exposures with the CK studies. SIGNIFICANCE: Selective inhibition of TARPγ8-containing AMPARs provided potent, dose-dependent antiseizure effects that were maintained after 7-day dosing in a preclinical focal seizure model. Exposures producing antiseizure effects did not impair motor function, yielding a high therapeutic index. RAP-219 may provide a new, regionally selective medicine for patients with epilepsy.

摘要：OBJECTIVE: The current study examined the occurrence, clinical predictors, and neuroanatomical correlates of self- or other-directed harmful ideation or behavior (HIB) following deep brain stimulation of the anterior nucleus of the thalamus (ANT-DBS) for drug-resistant epilepsy. METHODS: We retrospectively analyzed 90 ANT-DBS patients followed longitudinally, tracking stimulation parameters, seizure outcomes, and psychiatric status. HIB was defined as documented suicidal ideation, suicide attempt, homicidal ideation, threats, or physical aggression requiring clinical intervention. Preoperative predictors were identified using Firth penalized logistic regression. Lead reconstructions, volumes of tissue activated, sweet spot analysis, and normative structural and functional connectivity mapping were performed in Lead-DBS to compare patients with and without HIB. Stimulation parameters associated with symptom emergence and resolution were examined within patients longitudinally. RESULTS: Eleven of the 90 patients (12.2%) developed new or worsening HIB following ANT-DBS. Preoperative HIB predicted postoperative HIB (odds ratio = 8.07, p = .003). Neither the volume of tissue activated by stimulation nor the structural or functional connectivity of the stimulated tissue differed between patients who developed HIB and those who did not. Within patient analyses (n = 11), eight patients' symptoms were resolved by changing from high- to low-frequency stimulation, and three were resolved via contact and amplitude changes. SIGNIFICANCE: HIB following ANT-DBS occurs in a clinically meaningful minority of patients and is strongly associated with preoperative psychiatric vulnerability rather than differences in lead location or network engagement. Most cases were resolved through stimulation parameter adjustments, particularly frequency reduction, suggesting these complications are often reversible through routine DBS reprogramming. Careful preoperative psychiatric evaluation and close postoperative monitoring are recommended for patients undergoing ANT-DBS.

摘要：OBJECTIVE: To evaluate the longitudinal impact of epilepsy surgery on developmental trajectories in young children by comparing developmental rate (DR) during the early postoperative and later follow-up periods. METHODS: This single-center retrospective observational study included 90 children who underwent their first epilepsy surgery before 72 months of age. Development was assessed using the Kinder Infant Developmental Scale (KIDS). To reduce the influence of chronological age, DR was calculated as the change in developmental age divided by the observation period. DR during the first postoperative year was defined as early-phase DR, and DR from the 1-year postoperative assessment to the final developmental assessment was defined as late-phase DR. Developmental trajectories were compared between these two phases. RESULTS: Early-phase DR was significantly higher than late-phase DR in the overall cohort (median .22 vs .08, p < .001). This pattern was consistently observed regardless of epilepsy syndrome, surgical procedure, etiology, preoperative developmental quotient, epilepsy duration, or seizure recurrence during the first 12 postoperative months. Higher early-phase DR was associated with better preoperative developmental status and postoperative electroencephalographic improvement at 1 year. SIGNIFICANCE: Our findings suggest that postoperative developmental trajectories following epilepsy surgery in early childhood may be biphasic, characterized by an initial increase in developmental rate during the first postoperative year, followed by a slower but sustained increase thereafter. This early postoperative acceleration may partly reflect relief from epileptic encephalopathic effects, whereas later developmental attainment appears to be more strongly constrained by the underlying etiology and the child's intrinsic developmental potential. These findings should be interpreted as hypothesis generating and require validation using additional objective developmental measures.

摘要：OBJECTIVE: Sleep disorders are frequent but undercharacterized comorbidities in developmental and epileptic encephalopathies (DEEs). We aimed to estimate the frequency, spectrum, and burden of sleep disturbances across genetic DEE subtypes using real-world data extracted from electronic health records (EHRs) through natural language processing (NLP). METHODS: We conducted a retrospective cohort study using Dr. Warehouse, an NLP-enabled clinical data warehouse, at a tertiary pediatric center. Data were extracted from January to April 2025. Patients with a group of defined DEEs were identified from narrative clinical notes and matched 1:2 with pediatric neurology patients without history of epilepsy by age, follow-up duration, and document volume. Predefined sleep-related terms were extracted from free-text reports. The proportion of patients with ≥1 documented mention during follow-up was used to estimate occurrence, alongside multivariable logistic regression analyses and a cumulative sleep burden score. RESULTS: The final cohort included 884 patients with DEE and 1280 matched controls. At least one sleep-related disturbance was documented in 63.7% of DEE patients, significantly higher than in controls. Marked heterogeneity emerged across DEE subtypes; SYNGAP1-DEE showed the highest frequency of sleep disorders, Lennox-Gastaut syndrome was strongly associated with nocturnal seizures, and KCNT1- and PCDH19-DEE were more frequently associated with sleep apnea. DEE patients also exhibited a significantly higher cumulative burden of sleep disturbances compared with controls, with Lennox-Gastaut syndrome showing the greatest burden. SIGNIFICANCE: NLP-based mining of unstructured EHR data enables large-scale characterization of sleep disorders in DEEs, revealing DEE subtype-specific sleep phenotypes. Routine and etiology-informed assessment of sleep disturbances should be integrated into the clinical management of DEEs.

摘要：OBJECTIVE: Focal cortical dysplasia (FCD) is a common cause of drug-resistant epilepsy in children and adults and exhibits substantial clinical and histopathological heterogeneity. However, morphological classification alone does not fully explain variability in epileptogenicity, disease severity, or treatment response. METHODS: We aimed to define molecular mechanisms underlying FCD heterogeneity using integrated transcriptomic and regulatory analyses. RNA and small RNA sequencing (RNA-seq) were conducted on cortical tissue resected during epilepsy surgery from 66 patients with histopathologically confirmed FCD subtypes IA (n = 17), IIA (n = 25), and IIB (n = 24), along with 15 postmortem cortical control samples. Differential expression and weighted gene coexpression network analysis were used to identify coordinated transcriptional programs, and microRNA (miRNA) profiling was integrated to assess posttranscriptional regulatory mechanisms. Integrative multivariate analyses (DIABLO and sparse partial least squares discriminant analysis) were used to evaluate subtype discrimination, and findings were validated using an independent RNA-seq dataset. RESULTS: FCD subtypes showed distinct but partially overlapping molecular profiles. FCD IA showed a comparatively subtle transcriptional profile, dominated by reduced expression of genes involved in cortical development, neuronal signaling, ion regulation, and metabolic stress. FCD IIA displayed a modest yet coherent molecular signature involving developmental and neuronal disruption, together with metabolic stress and autophagy. FCD IIB exhibited the most extensive transcriptional remodeling, including activation of antigen presentation pathways, complement activation, cytoskeleton reorganization, synaptic alterations, and extracellular matrix organization. Differential miRNA profiling identified shared miRNAs, including miR-320 family, miR-27a-5p, miR-142-5p, and miR-21-5p, alongside subtype-specific regulatory patterns. METHODS: Patients with TSC and MRE who underwent MEG, stereo-EEG (sEEG) monitoring, and have at least 1-year postsurgical follow-up were included. Sustained seizure freedom after diagnostic implantation alone is rarely described. 摘要：PURPOSE: Continuous electroencephalography (cEEG) is the gold standard for detecting electrographic seizures in the intensive care unit (ICU), but limited resources limit prolonged monitoring for all patients. We developed an automated, interpretable pipeline that uses a single hour of cEEG to estimate near-term seizure risk across multiple prediction windows. METHODS: We retrospectively studied 50 adults who underwent ICU cEEG monitoring at a single tertiary center. From a 1-hour segment, we computed automated interictal-ictal continuum (IIC) burden probabilities for lateralized periodic discharges (LPDs), generalized periodic discharges (GPDs), and lateralized rhythmic delta activity (LRDA), along with quantitative background EEG features reflecting signal organization and hemispheric asymmetry. Segments were labeled by seizure occurrence within the subsequent 1, 2, 4, 8, 12, or 24 hours. Support vector machine (SVM) and bagged-tree classifiers were trained using clinical-only, background-only, and combined feature sets with a 70/30 patient-level train-test split and cross-validation within the training set. RESULTS: For the 4-hour horizon, the combined-feature SVM achieved 76% accuracy and the lowest Brier score (0.25), outperforming clinical-only (72%) and background-only (70%) models. Across horizons, SVM accuracy declined from 94% (1 hour) to 79% (24 hours) with increasing Brier scores, indicating reduced calibration at longer intervals. AUC-receiver operating characteristic increased with wider prediction windows, consistent with higher seizure prevalence at longer lead times. CONCLUSIONS: Integrating automated IIC probabilities with quantitative background EEG features enables moderate, interpretable near-term seizure prediction from a single hour of ICU cEEG. Initial external validation on an independent multicenter ICU cohort supports generalizability, although broader prospective and multicenter evaluation are needed before clinical deployment.

摘要：Resting-state EEG studies in Parkinson's disease (PD) have largely emphasized static power or connectivity, whereas fixed-sequence OFF-to-ON session differences may also involve the temporal organization of network states. Paired source-space evidence under such fixed acquisition protocols remains limited. We analyzed 64-channel eyes-closed resting-state EEG in 39 patients with PD contributing paired OFF/ON recordings for the prespecified 90 s primary analysis; 24 also contributed paired 178 s recordings for duration robustness. After individual MRI-based source reconstruction, low-beta (13-20 Hz) dynamic amplitude-envelope connectivity was computed with 2 s windows. Primary endpoints were switching rate (SR) and normalized Shannon entropy (H); fractional occupancy (FO_path) and mean dwell time (MDT_path) of an internally defined OFF-dominant state were prespecified secondary conditional endpoints and are interpreted only as conditional summaries. PD-only linear mixed-effects models quantified the fixed-sequence OFF-to-ON session contrast; medication state and session order were necessarily collinear. In the 90 s analysis, the later ON session showed higher SR (β = 3.3224, Holm p = 0.00406) and H (β = 0.06781, Holm p = 0.000749) than the earlier OFF session. FO_path (β = -0.09167, Holm p = 1.08 × 10⁻⁵) and MDT_path (β = -1.05171, Holm p = 0.00524) were lower in the later ON session. The 178 s reanalysis preserved the same directions, although SR and especially MDT_path were more parameter-sensitive across sensitivity analyses. The fixed OFF-then-ON protocol was associated with reproducible within-subject differences in low-beta state dynamics, with the strongest evidence arising from SR and H. Because medication state was perfectly collinear with session order, these findings cannot distinguish medication-specific effects from order-, time-, vigilance-, fatigue-, or adaptation-related influences. FO_path and MDT_path remain conditional summaries tied to an internally defined OFF-dominant state, and MDT was less stable in the 178 s and OFF-only sensitivity analyses.
