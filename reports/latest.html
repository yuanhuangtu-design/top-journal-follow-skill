摘要：IMPORTANCE: Late-onset epilepsy (LOE; onset at age ≥65 years) is increasingly recognized in older adults and may be associated with vascular risk. OBJECTIVE: To identify vascular risk factors (VRFs) associated with incident LOE in older adults. DESIGN, SETTING, AND PARTICIPANTS: This study is an individual participant data meta-analysis of 6 US population-based prospective cohort studies (the Epilepsy-Cog Study). In 5 cohorts linked to Medicare for epilepsy case ascertainment, follow-up for LOE began when a participant had accrued 2 years of continuous fee-for-service enrollment and was 65 years of age or older and extended to the end of continuous enrollment. Claims linkage covered 1991-2018 in the Atherosclerosis Risk in Communities Study (ARIC), 1992-2019 in the Cardiovascular Health Study (CHS), 2000-2019 in the Multi-Ethnic Study of Atherosclerosis (MESA), 2000-2020 in the Reasons for Geographic and Racial Differences in Stroke Study (REGARDS), and 1999-2019 in the Washington Heights/Inwood Columbia Aging Project (WHICAP). In the Northern Manhattan Study (NOMAS), which was not Medicare linked and used cohort-based epilepsy case ascertainment, follow-up for LOE began at cohort entry in 1993-2008 and extended to 2024. Data analysis was completed in January 2026. EXPOSURES: Sociodemographic characteristics and VRFs assessed by self-report, examinations, laboratory measures, and medication use. MAIN OUTCOME AND MEASURES: The primary outcome was incident LOE. Associations between risk factors and incident LOE were evaluated using multivariable Cox proportional hazards models to estimate hazard ratios. RESULTS: From 43 753 participants in Epilepsy-Cog, 551 participants with prevalent epilepsy and 945 with no follow-up after Epilepsy-Cog baseline were excluded. Among 42 257 participants (mean [SD] age, 71.4 [6.9] years; 23 383 [56.0%] female) followed up for a mean (SD) of 9.3 (6.2) years, 1500 participants (3.5%) developed LOE (3.8 per 1000 person-years). In fully adjusted models, LOE risk was higher with current smoking (HR = 1.39; 95% CI, 1.18-1.64), hypertension (HR = 1.17; 95% CI, 1.04-1.31), diabetes (HR = 1.42; 95% CI, 1.25-1.62), chronic kidney disease (HR = 1.29; 95% CI, 1.13-1.48), history of stroke (HR = 1.99; 95% CI, 1.63-2.43), and apolipoprotein E ε4 allele (1 ε4 allele: HR = 1.22; 95% CI, 1.07-1.39; 2 ε4 alleles: HR = 1.84; 95% CI, 1.36-2.50). Associations of VRFs with LOE were similar across age, sex, and race and ethnicity subgroups. CONCLUSIONS AND RELEVANCE: In this pooled analysis of 6 US cohorts, multiple VRFs and a history of stroke were associated with incident LOE. These findings suggest that LOE may reflect the underlying burden of brain vascular disease.

摘要：PURPOSE: Temporal lobe epilepsy (TLE) is increasingly conceptualized as a network disorder, with the piriform cortex as an implicated region. We characterized postoperative contralateral piriform cortex-cortical amygdala (PCA) structural and perfusion-related changes in a small seizure-free TLE cohort. METHODS: Nine patients with drug-resistant TLE who underwent epilepsy surgery and achieved postoperative seizure freedom were included in this single-center pre-post pilot study. Bilateral PCA was manually delineated on 3D T1-weighted MRI using ITK-SNAP, and volumes were normalized to total intracranial volume derived from CAT12 segmentation. Eight atlas-derived contralateral temporal lobe regions were examined as exploratory regional-specificity comparisons. In patients with available pulsed arterial spin labeling (PASL), piriform and putaminal signal intensities were measured by region-of-interest analysis. Non-parametric paired tests were used; secondary and exploratory analyses were corrected using the Benjamini-Hochberg false discovery rate (FDR) procedure. RESULTS: Normalized to total intracranial volume (TIV) contralateral PCA volume decreased postoperatively (Wilcoxon Z = -2.073, exact two-tailed p = 0.039; r = 0.69). None of the eight atlas-derived contralateral temporal regions showed a significant pre-post volume change. Contralateral putamen-normalized piriform signal intensity did not change significantly (Z = -0.105, p = 0.917), and no secondary or exploratory analysis remained significant after FDR correction. CONCLUSIONS: In this hypothesis-generating pilot cohort, contralateral PCA volume decreased after epilepsy surgery, whereas the PASL-derived signal-intensity measure did not show a corresponding group-level change. These observations do not establish a surgery-induced network mechanism and require confirmation in larger controlled longitudinal cohorts.

摘要：BACKGROUND: Drug-resistant epilepsy (DRE) remains a high-morbidity condition despite expanding antiseizure medications. Acetazolamide (ACZ), a carbonic anhydrase inhibitor introduced for epilepsy in the 1950s, has been largely displaced by newer agents and concerns about tolerance, but its low cost and pH-mediated mechanism support reassessment in select DRE contexts. METHODS: We conducted a PROSPERO-registered, PRISMA-S-informed systematic review of clinical studies evaluating ACZ as mono- or adjunctive therapy for drug-resistant or refractory epilepsy through May 11, 2026. Risk of bias was assessed using Newcastle-Ottawa and ROBINS-I frameworks. To quantify treatment efficacy random-effects generalized linear mixed-model meta-analyses estimated seizure freedom and ≥50% responder rate. RESULTS: Twenty-six studies were included. The pooled seizure-freedom proportion was 25.7% (95% CI, 15.8%-38.9%; k = 20; I² = 89.5%) and ≥50% responder proportion 67.5% (95% CI, 48.2%-82.2%; k = 26; I² = 96.7%). Leave-one-out analysis did not identify a single influential study nor did exploratory meta-regression show a significant age-response correlation. Phenotype-specific and narrative findings demonstrated more consistent response in certain catamenial, spike-wave, photosensitive, genetic, and metabolic epilepsy contexts. Metabolic acidosis, nephrolithiasis, and treatment discontinuation were comparatively uncommon, whereas adverse effects were predominantly tolerability-related. CONCLUSION: ACZ retains a measurable but selective antiseizure signal in DRE. However, limitations in decades-old reporting, considerable between-study heterogeneity, limited controlled clinical evidence, and modest sample sizes in phenotype-specific cohorts require researchers to interpret the evidence with caution. Nevertheless, ACZ's observed clinical activity, low cost, and distinct mechanism of action make it a promising candidate for prospective studies to identify responsive epilepsy types, assess the durability of benefit, and redefine its place in modern epilepsy care.

摘要：OBJECTIVE: This study was undertaken to evaluate seizure outcomes after magnetic resonance-guided laser interstitial thermal therapy (MRgLITT) in children with insular epilepsy and to assess how the extent of ablation overlaps with multimodal definitions of the ictal onset zone (IOZ). METHODS: We conducted a retrospective, single-center study including consecutive pediatric patients treated with MRgLITT for insular epilepsy. All patients underwent presurgical evaluation including electroencephalography (EEG) and magnetic resonance imaging (MRI); fluorodeoxyglucose positron emission tomography (FDG-PET) and stereo-EEG (SEEG) were performed in negative-MRI cases. Epileptic zone was identified by visual analysis of SEEG recording. Postablation contrast-enhancing volumes were manually segmented and coregistered with IOZ estimates derived from Multi-centre Epilepsy Lesion Detection (MELD) MRI analysis, FDG-PET voxel-based analysis, and the Epileptogenicity Index, when available. Spatial concordance between modalities was assessed using Dice similarity coefficients, and the proportion of each IOZ modality encompassed within the ablation volume was correlated with postoperative seizure outcomes (International League Against Epilepsy classification). RESULTS: Eight children (median age = 10.4 years, range = 2.3-17) were included, with a median postoperative follow-up of 38 months. Two patients had insular tumors, and both achieved seizure freedom after complete ablation. Among the six MRI-negative patients, FDG-PET revealed insular hypometabolism in four, MELD detected a median of one insular cluster (range 1-3), and SEEG identified focal insular ictal activity in three cases. Overall, four negative-MRI patients achieved seizure freedom: three after MRgLITT alone and one after MRgLITT combined with frontal opercular resection. One transient postoperative aphasia was observed. Intermodality spatial concordance was low across all comparisons. A greater proportion of FDG-PET hypometabolism included in the ablation volume was associated with better seizure outcomes (p = .012).
